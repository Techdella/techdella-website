AI search questions

What are AEO and GEO?+
Answer engine optimization and generative engine optimization. Both describe making your brand easy for AI tools to understand, trust and recommend.
Can you guarantee ChatGPT will mention us?+
No. AI answers change from query to query. We improve the signals AI relies on and report your share of mentions honestly over time.
How do you measure AI visibility?+
We track a fixed set of buyer prompts across AI platforms every month and report the share of answers that mention or cite you, alongside traffic and leads.
Do we still need SEO?+
Yes. Many AI answers draw on ranked web pages, so strong SEO makes AI optimization more effective.
