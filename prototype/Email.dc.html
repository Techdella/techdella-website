Email marketing questions

Which email platform should we use?+
We work with the one you have. If you’re starting fresh, we usually recommend HubSpot for B2B sales teams, or Brevo for smaller budgets.
Do you write and design the emails?+
Yes. Strategy, copy, templates and design are all handled by our team.
Can you fix emails going to spam?+
We set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC, clean your list and adjust sending habits, which resolves most deliverability problems.
How do you measure success?+
Replies, meetings booked and deals influenced, alongside open and click rates.
