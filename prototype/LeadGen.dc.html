Lead generation questions

What counts as a qualified lead?+
We define it with you before we start: company size, role, need and timing. Only leads that match are counted and passed to sales.
Do you buy or sell lead lists?+
No. We build your own pipeline through offers, content, ads and personalized outreach.
How quickly will we see leads?+
Paid and outbound can start producing leads soon after launch; SEO-driven leads build over months. We’ll set realistic expectations in your plan.
Which CRM do you work with?+
HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho and most CRMs with an API. If you don’t have one, we’ll help you set it up.
