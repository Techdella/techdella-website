THE ARCH
Portraits and feature images sit in arch frames; large outline arches can mark dark sections.
✓ Do Use one orange moment per section: a label dot, the button arrow, or a single highlighted number. Let photography bring warmth. Our team portraits already carry the brand’s arches and tones. Use black for primary buttons; it reads more premium and keeps orange meaningful.
✕ Don’t Fill large backgrounds, cards or headlines with orange or red. Put white text on #E8470A. It fails contrast; use ink, or #C23B07 for orange text. Mix in other accent colors. Status colors (green for success) are the only exception.
