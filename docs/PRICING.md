# Pricing (set 11 Oct 2026)

The owner asked for market-standard, competitive prices. These are **starting points in US dollars**;
each client's scope sets the final quote, agreed in writing before work starts (pricing FAQ). They sit
in the lower-middle of published market ranges: below US agency medians, above freelancer rates,
which suits a senior team serving startups and SMBs in Nigeria, the US and beyond.
