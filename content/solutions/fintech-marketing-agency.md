compliance_notes: |
  - Google Ads: the Financial services policy requires advertisers in many countries to complete financial services verification, and crypto exchanges and wallets need separate certification under the Cryptocurrencies and related products policy.
  - Meta: ads for credit and other financial products run in a special ad category, which removes targeting by age, gender and detailed demographics. Plan creative and audiences for broad delivery.
  - UK: financial promotions must be approved or made by an FCA-authorised firm under section 21 of FSMA, and the Consumer Duty requires communications that consumers can understand. Influencer posts count as promotions too.
  - US and Nigeria: credit ads that quote rates or terms trigger Truth in Lending (Regulation Z) disclosures, and the CFPB enforces against unfair or deceptive practices. In Nigeria, CBN consumer protection rules and SEC Nigeria advertising rules for investment products apply. Comparison and affiliate content must disclose paid relationships under FTC endorsement rules.

A fintech marketing agency helps payments, lending, wallet and comparison companies win customers who are careful with money: it earns trust on your site, ranks you for the questions people ask before they sign up and runs ads that clear financial verification. We work in that order. We make the value and the safeguards obvious in the first two sentences of your key pages, build [fintech SEO](/services/seo/) and [AI search](/services/ai-search-optimization/) content around the queries people use to compare financial tools, launch [paid campaigns](/services/paid-ads/) only after verification and compliance review, and use [onboarding email](/services/email-marketing/) to recover users who stall at KYC. New to the field? Our glossary entry on [fintech marketing](/glossary/fintech-marketing/) covers the basics.

Two published case studies show the approach. With [Paycape](/case-studies/paycape/), a fintech platform for Nigerian businesses, we refreshed positioning on core pages and built SEO content clusters anchored in real comparison queries, with internal links leading readers toward demo pages. Over a five-month engagement in Q3 2025, Paycape reached its first result in 50 days, grew to around 15,000 monthly organic visitors and saw 50% of conversions come from inbound. With [Havyn](/case-studies/havyn/), which holds payments until delivery is confirmed, we rewrote homepage and support copy to explain the problem before the product and made next steps explicit.
