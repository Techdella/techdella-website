For fintech, trust comes before traffic. We start by making the value and the safeguards obvious in the first two sentences of your key pages, then build search content around the questions people ask when they compare financial tools. Paid campaigns come after verification and compliance review, and onboarding email recovers users who stall at KYC.

Two published case studies show the approach. With Paycape, a fintech insights and comparison platform, we refreshed positioning on core pages and built SEO content clusters anchored in real comparison queries, with internal links leading readers toward demo pages. Over a five-month engagement in Q3 2025, Paycape reached its first result in 50 days, grew to around 15,000 monthly organic visitors and saw 50% of conversions come from inbound. With Havyn, which holds payments until delivery is confirmed, we rewrote homepage and support copy to explain the problem before the product and made next steps explicit.
