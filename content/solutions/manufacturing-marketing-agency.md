A manufacturing marketing agency helps manufacturers and industrial suppliers get found by engineers and procurement teams, then turns that attention into RFQs worth quoting. We fix the [technical SEO](/services/technical-seo/) that hides product data, write capability pages with your engineers, and rebuild the RFQ path with [lead generation](/services/lead-generation/) and [automated follow-up](/services/marketing-automation/). We judge the work by quotes and won jobs, not traffic.

1. **Restructure the site around capabilities and applications.** We move key product and process data out of PDFs into indexable pages. Engineers search for specifics, and search engines cannot rank what they cannot read.
2. **Write application pages with your engineers.** We interview your team and turn their answers into pages for specific materials, processes and industries. These match the narrow, high-intent searches that come before an RFQ.
3. **Rebuild the RFQ form to qualify buyers.** We ask for quantity, material, timeline and drawings up front. Sales then spends time quoting real jobs instead of sorting enquiries.
4. **Connect events and downloads to follow-up.** Trade show contacts and spec-sheet downloads go into a tagged, automated sequence. Industrial buying cycles are long, and steady contact keeps you on the shortlist.
5. **Review results with sales every month.** We track RFQs and quote rates by source, not traffic. That shows which pages and events bring work you want.

For named target accounts, we pair search with [account-based marketing](/glossary/account-based-marketing/) and [LinkedIn Thought Leader Ads](/glossary/linkedin-thought-leader-ads/) once the RFQ path converts.
