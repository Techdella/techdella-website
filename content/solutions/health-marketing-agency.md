compliance_notes: |
  - HIPAA: covered entities and their business associates cannot share protected health information with ad platforms without authorization. HHS OCR guidance on online tracking technologies explains when pixels and analytics on patient-facing pages create risk. Sign BAAs with vendors that touch PHI.
  - Non-HIPAA health apps: the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule covers many consumer health apps, and the FTC has acted against companies that shared health data with advertisers. Health claims also need competent and reliable scientific evidence.
  - Google Ads: the Healthcare and medicines policy restricts many health products, prescription drug ads need certification, and personalized advertising rules block targeting based on health conditions.
  - Meta and other markets: Meta's personal attributes rules ban ads that assert or imply a person's health condition. In the UK, the CAP Code and MHRA rules govern health and medicine claims; in Nigeria, NAFDAC approval applies to regulated products.

1. **Audit every tag and form for health data.** We check which pixels, analytics and chat tools fire on booking, login and symptom pages. A privacy problem costs more than any campaign earns.
2. **Fix the patient or buyer path on key pages.** We make it clear who the service is for, what happens next and how to book or request a demo. Health decisions carry anxiety, and unclear steps make people leave.
3. **Set up a clinical review workflow for content.** Every article gets a qualified author, a reviewer, sources and a last-reviewed date. Google holds health content to its highest quality bar.
4. **Build search content around real patient and provider questions.** We start with questions close to a decision, such as how a treatment or service works. These bring visitors who are ready to act.
5. **Add consent-based email and privacy-safe paid campaigns.** Once the basics are sound, we grow with channels that do not depend on targeting health conditions. That keeps campaigns approved and patients' trust intact.

We don't have a published healthcare case study yet, so we will be clear about what we expect and measure it with you from week one.
