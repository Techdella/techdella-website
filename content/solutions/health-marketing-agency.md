A healthcare marketing agency helps clinics, healthtech companies and digital health products reach patients and buyers without breaking privacy or advertising rules. We start with the tracking and consent on your site, then build clinically reviewed [search content](/services/seo/), [local SEO](/services/local-seo/) for each location and campaigns that never target a health condition. Every step is agreed with your privacy lead, because a tracking mistake costs more than any campaign earns.

1. **Audit every tag and form for health data.** We check which pixels, analytics and chat tools fire on booking, login and symptom pages. A privacy problem costs more than any campaign earns.
2. **Fix the patient or buyer path on key pages.** We make it clear who the service is for, what happens next and how to book or request a demo. Health decisions carry anxiety, and unclear steps make people leave.
3. **Set up a clinical review workflow for content.** Every article gets a qualified author, a reviewer, sources and a last-reviewed date. Google holds health content to its highest quality bar.
4. **Build search content around real patient and provider questions.** We start with questions close to a decision, such as how a treatment or service works. These bring visitors who are ready to act.
5. **Add consent-based email and privacy-safe paid campaigns.** Once the basics are sound, we grow with channels that do not depend on targeting health conditions. That keeps campaigns approved and patients' trust intact.

The rules we plan around: the HHS [bulletin on online tracking technologies](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/hipaa-online-tracking/index.html) (partly vacated by a federal court in June 2024), the FTC [Health Breach Notification Rule](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/04/ftc-finalizes-changes-health-breach-notification-rule) for apps outside HIPAA, Google's [Healthcare and medicines policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031) and Meta's [personal attributes policy](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/privacy-violations-personal-attributes/). For measurement that keeps sensitive data out of ad platforms, see our notes on [server-side tracking](/glossary/server-side-tracking/) and [Consent Mode v2](/glossary/consent-mode-v2/).
