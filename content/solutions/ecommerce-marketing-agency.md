For ecommerce, we judge every channel by revenue, not visits. We start with the path from first view to checkout: whether the product page answers price, delivery and returns, and whether email flows catch people who leave. Then we scale paid social and Shopping with structured creative tests, and add SEO for the category and buying-guide searches marketplaces leave open.

Two published case studies show this in practice. With Shopinbos, an ecommerce toolkit for first-time sellers, we started from one question: what should a new seller do in their first five minutes? We clarified landing messages around a concrete seller problem, sequenced the steps from discovering products to choosing suppliers to selling, and reviewed calls to action to reduce hesitation. With BlueCart, a B2B ordering platform for restaurants and suppliers, we repositioned the product as a single place to order, track inventory and manage spend, and aligned messaging around outcomes like fewer ordering errors and time saved.
