A real estate marketing agency helps developers, agencies and proptech platforms attract buyer, renter and investor leads who are ready to act, then follow up across a decision that can take months. We start with [local SEO](/services/local-seo/) and qualifying enquiry forms, add [automated follow-up](/services/marketing-automation/) and [email](/services/email-marketing/), and run paid campaigns inside each platform's housing ad rules.

1. **Add qualifying questions to every enquiry form.** We ask about budget, timeline, location and financing up front. Agents can then call ready buyers first instead of working through a list of browsers.
2. **Set up instant replies and viewing booking.** Every enquiry gets an immediate response and a way to book a viewing or call. Property leads cool quickly when the first reply takes a day.
3. **Build area and development pages for local search.** We create pages for the neighborhoods and projects you sell. Most property searches include a place, and these pages match them.
4. **Rebuild ad campaigns within housing rules.** We move targeting to location, property interest and search intent, with creative that does the qualifying. This keeps campaigns compliant and still reaches serious buyers.
5. **Start a long-cycle nurture sequence.** Matching listings, market updates and development news go out on a steady cadence. Buyers and investors take months to decide, and steady contact keeps you in the running.

The rules we plan around: the US [Fair Housing Act ban on discriminatory housing ads](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/3604), Meta's [special ad category rules](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/audiences/special-ad-category), Google's [policy for housing, employment and credit ads](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/9997418), National Trading Standards [material information guidance](https://www.nationaltradingstandards.uk/news/material-information-for-property-listings-announced/) for UK listings, and the SEC Nigeria [warning on unregistered crowdfunding platforms](https://home.sec.gov.ng/for-investors/keep-track-of-circulars/re-warning-against-investing-with-unregistered-investments-crowdfunding-platforms/).
