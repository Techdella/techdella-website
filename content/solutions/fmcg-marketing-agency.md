An FMCG marketing agency helps consumer goods brands build demand that shows up on the shelf and on marketplaces, and gives them a direct line to shoppers that retailers do not provide. We run structured creative tests on [paid social](/services/paid-ads/), including creator and [user-generated content](/glossary/user-generated-content/), turn every promotion into a consented list through [lead generation](/services/lead-generation/), and keep buyers coming back with [repeat-purchase email](/services/email-marketing/). Measurement is agreed before spend rises, so digital budgets can be defended. Our usual order:

1. **Turn every promotion into a list builder.** We add consented sign-up to samples, competitions and on-pack QR codes. Without first-party data, each campaign starts from zero.
2. **Set up a structured creative testing plan.** We test hooks, formats and offers on short-form video in small batches. Consumer goods win on creative, and testing shows what sells before budgets scale.
3. **Agree how digital impact on retail will be measured.** We set up geo or time-based tests, promo codes and marketplace tracking before spend increases. Budgets survive when impact can be shown.
4. **Launch a simple repeat-purchase email program.** Usage ideas, refill reminders and loyalty offers go to subscribers on a steady schedule. Repeat purchases protect margins better than deeper discounts.
5. **Clear regulatory approvals early.** We build ARCON, NAFDAC or other market approvals into the campaign calendar. Late approval is the most common reason consumer launches slip.
