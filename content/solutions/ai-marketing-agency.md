For AI products and digital entertainment, belief and activation come before reach. We translate what the technology does into a plain outcome, make product facts easy for AI assistants to cite, and design the first session around one measurable action. Automation and email then bring back users who stall, and paid video comes in once new users stick.

Two published case studies show the approach. With Epic Entertainment Games, a distributor of game credits and casino software platforms, we mapped how credit requests moved between teams, then built a unified workflow with a partner onboarding flow, a central status dashboard and automated alerts for exceptions instead of manual checks. With Lykluk, a platform for creators sharing African cultural stories through video and commerce, we tightened the narrative so every message answered what users get and what to do next, and tied content to actions like sign-up, profile completion and first upload.
