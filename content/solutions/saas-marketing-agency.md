compliance_notes: |
  - Reviews and testimonials: the FTC's Trade Regulation Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (2024) bans fake reviews and buying positive ones. Incentives for G2 or Capterra reviews must be disclosed and offered regardless of sentiment.
  - B2B email: in the US, CAN-SPAM requires a working opt-out, a postal address and honest subject lines. In the UK, PECR allows email to corporate addresses with an opt-out, but sole traders and some partnerships count as individuals and need consent.
  - EU and UK prospects: outbound and enrichment data fall under GDPR. Keep a legitimate interest assessment, a clear privacy notice and a fast route to object.
  - Security claims: badges like SOC 2 or ISO 27001 on marketing pages must match a current report or certificate. “GDPR compliant” and “bank-grade security” claims need to be ones you can back up.

A SaaS marketing agency helps software companies get onto buyers' shortlists and turn trials into paying accounts. We start where buyers decide: the problem statement on your site and the comparison queries they search before a demo. We map your motion first (product-led, sales-led or both), then build [SaaS SEO](/services/seo/) and [AI search](/services/ai-search-optimization/) content around real buying questions, and an [onboarding email](/services/email-marketing/) sequence that moves trial users to their first useful outcome. [Automation](/services/marketing-automation/) ties product events to [lead scoring](/glossary/lead-scoring/), so sales calls the accounts that are ready. Paid search and LinkedIn come last, once the funnel converts, so ad spend is not filling a leak.

You can see this approach in two published case studies. With [WezOps](/case-studies/wezops/), an HR operations partner, we turned what the team already knew into problem-first language: a reusable case study framework, rewritten website pages and tighter sales decks. With [startbuddi](/case-studies/startbuddi/), we shaped positioning around operational simplicity, leading with outcomes like fewer manual tasks and easier client management instead of software jargon, and carried that through onboarding.
