A small business marketing agency helps a small team get found locally, turn website visits into enquiries and follow up without extra admin. We fix the path from visit to enquiry before buying more traffic: a message where the right customer sees themselves on the first screen, one clear next step on every page, and simple [automated follow-up](/services/marketing-automation/) so leads do not wait for a free hour. [Local SEO](/services/local-seo/) and [email to past customers](/services/email-marketing/) come next, because they cost the least for a small team.

Two published case studies show this approach. With the [Angie Diedricks Suicide Prevention Program](/case-studies/angie-diedricks/), a nonprofit supporting adolescents in South Africa, we reworked a site that read like an information page into a clear support pathway, so young people and families could see who it was for and how to get help. With [Three Cat Agency](/case-studies/three-cat-agency/), a startup-focused marketing agency, we fixed a leaky funnel by guiding visitors to a decision, making offers easier to explain, and setting up tracking to show what was driving results.

The rules we keep to: Google's [Business Profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) and the [Google Ad Grants](https://www.google.com/grants/) terms for nonprofits. If you are new to the basics, start with our notes on [local SEO](/glossary/local-seo/) and the [call to action](/glossary/call-to-action-cta/).
