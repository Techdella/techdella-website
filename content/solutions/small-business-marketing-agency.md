For small businesses, we fix the path from visit to enquiry before buying more traffic. We tighten the message so the right customer sees themselves on the first screen, give every page one clear next step, and set up simple automated follow-up so leads do not wait for a free hour. Local search and email to past customers come next, because they cost the least for a small team.

Two published case studies show this approach. With the Angie Diedricks Suicide Prevention Program, a nonprofit supporting adolescents in South Africa, we reworked a site that read like an information page into a clear support pathway, so young people and families could see who it was for and how to get help. With Three Cat Agency, a startup-focused marketing agency, we fixed a leaky funnel by guiding visitors to a decision, making offers easier to explain, and setting up tracking to show what was driving results.
