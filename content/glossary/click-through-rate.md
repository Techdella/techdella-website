Click-through rate, or CTR, tells you how often people who see your link actually click it. If your page appears in Google 1,000 times and gets 50 clicks, its CTR is 5%. The same idea applies to ads, emails and buttons. CTR is a measure of how appealing and relevant your listing is to the people who see it.

:::takeaways
- CTR = clicks ÷ impressions × 100.
- Organic CTR depends heavily on position and on what else is on the results page, including AI summaries and ads.
- Compare CTR for similar queries and positions, not across your whole site.
- Titles, meta descriptions and rich results are the main levers you control in organic search.
:::

## How to calculate CTR

The formula is the same everywhere:

**CTR = (clicks ÷ impressions) × 100**

Search Console uses exactly this definition. In email, "impressions" is usually replaced by emails delivered or opened, so check which base your tool uses before comparing numbers.

### What a CTR change is worth

Here's an illustrative example. A guide gets 20,000 impressions a month at a 2% CTR, which is 400 clicks. Rewriting the title and description lifts CTR to 3%: 600 clicks. If 2% of visitors become leads, that's 8 leads before and 12 after, from the same ranking. Small CTR gains on high-impression pages are often the cheapest wins in SEO.

## Benchmarks, with caveats

Benchmarks vary by industry, query type and SERP layout, so use them as context, not targets.

:::stats "CTR benchmarks worth knowing" highlight=0
27.6% | average CTR of the first organic result in Backlinko's analysis of 4 million Google results | Backlinko, updated 2025 | https://backlinko.com/google-ctr-stats
6.64% | average CTR for search ads across industries | LocaliQ search advertising benchmarks, 2026 | https://localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/
8% vs 15% | share of visits with a click on a regular result, with and without an AI summary on the page | Pew Research Center, 2025 | https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/
:::

The Pew finding matters for 2026 planning. When an AI summary appears, fewer people click any result. If a page's impressions hold steady but its clicks fall, check whether an AI Overview now appears for its main queries.

## Why CTR matters

In organic search, CTR turns visibility into visits. In paid search, Google uses expected CTR as one of the three components of Quality Score, the diagnostic tool for ad quality. In email, CTR shows whether your content and [calls to action](/glossary/call-to-action-cta/) actually move people.

:::myth-fact
Myth: A high CTR is always good.
Fact: Clickbait titles can raise CTR while visitors leave immediately and don't convert. A good CTR brings the right people, so watch conversions on the same page.
Myth: Your site-wide average CTR is a useful number.
Fact: It mixes branded and non-branded queries and every position. Segment by query type and position before drawing conclusions.
:::

## How to improve click-through rate

:::steps "Raise organic CTR in a month"
1. Find the opportunities | In Search Console, filter pages with high impressions, positions 1 to 10, and CTR below similar pages.
2. Match the intent in the title | Put the searcher's words near the start. Promise exactly what the page delivers.
3. Write a real meta description | Summarize the answer and the benefit in about 150 characters. Google may rewrite it, but a good one is often used.
4. Earn rich results | Add valid structured data such as breadcrumbs, products or articles. See [schema markup](/glossary/schema-markup/).
5. Add specifics | Numbers, years and concrete outcomes ("formula and examples") tend to stand out more than vague claims.
6. Measure in four weeks | Compare CTR at a similar average position before and after. Keep what works.
:::

For paid ads, the same thinking applies: match the keyword in the headline, use ad assets, and test variations. For emails, test subject lines; our free [email subject line analyzer](/tools/email-subject-line-analyzer/) gives quick feedback. For a rigorous way to compare versions, see [A/B testing](/glossary/a-b-testing/).

:::faq
### What is a good click-through rate?
It depends on the channel and position. For search ads, the cross-industry average in LocaliQ's 2026 data was 6.64%. For organic results, CTR falls steeply by position. Compare each page with your own pages at similar positions for similar query types.
### Why is my CTR low even though I rank well?
Common reasons are a title that doesn't match the query, an unappealing snippet, ads and AI summaries pushing your result down, or rich results on competitors' listings. Also check whether the query is informational and fully answered on the results page.
### Does CTR affect Google rankings?
Google doesn't confirm CTR as a direct ranking factor for organic results. Treat CTR as a measure of how well your listing earns attention. In Google Ads, expected CTR is part of the Quality Score diagnostic.
### How is email CTR different from click-to-open rate?
Email CTR usually divides unique clicks by emails delivered. Click-to-open rate divides clicks by opens, so it measures how well the email content works once opened. Privacy features that inflate opens make click-based metrics more reliable.
:::
