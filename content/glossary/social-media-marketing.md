Social media marketing is the use of social platforms to reach the people you want as customers, build trust with them and move them to act. It has two halves: **organic** (posts, comments and community, unpaid) and **paid** (ads targeted by job, interest or behavior). The best social media marketing uses organic to earn credibility and paid to reach the right people at scale, with both pointing at a clear next step.

:::takeaways
- Pick platforms by where your buyers pay attention, not by which is popular this year.
- Short video is the format marketers rate highest for return right now.
- Tie every campaign to a business outcome such as leads, demos or sales, not follower growth.
:::

## Why social media marketing matters

Social platforms are where most of the world spends its online attention, and where buyers check whether a business is credible.

## Which platforms suit which goals

| Platform | Strongest for | Typical B2B use |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Reaching decision makers by role and company | Thought leadership, lead gen forms, account targeting |
| YouTube | Search-driven video and long explainers | Product demos, tutorials, webinars |
| Instagram | Visual brands, culture and hiring | Behind the scenes, short video, proof |
| TikTok | Fast reach with short video | Founder stories, explainers, younger buyers |
| Facebook | Broad reach, groups, retargeting | Small business audiences, remarketing |
| X | Real-time conversation in tech and finance | Commentary, community, support |

Most B2B teams start with LinkedIn plus one video platform, then add paid campaigns once organic shows which messages resonate.

## How organic and paid work together

[Organic social media](/glossary/organic-social-media/) tests messages cheaply and builds the credibility people check before buying. Paid social puts the winners in front of exactly the audience you choose, and retargets people who visited your site. Running ads from an empty or inactive profile wastes money, because curious prospects click through to your page and leave.

:::myth-fact
Myth: B2B buyers don't make decisions on social media.
Fact: Few B2B deals close on social, but many start there. Buyers notice brands, follow experts and check credibility on social long before they fill in a form.
:::

## How to do social media marketing

:::steps "Six steps from goal to results"
1. Set a business goal | For example, 30 demo requests a month from operations leaders at logistics firms.
2. Choose one or two platforms | Match them to where that audience spends time and how they research.
3. Plan content pillars | Three or four recurring themes tied to buyer problems, each with a clear point of view.
4. Make platform-native creative | Vertical video, carousels and document posts. Our [creative brief template](/resources/paid-ads-creative-brief-template/) keeps ad briefs consistent.
5. Amplify what works | Put budget behind organic posts that already earn comments from your audience.
6. Measure and adjust monthly | Track cost per lead, lead quality and pipeline by platform. Cut what doesn't move them.
:::

:::faq
### What does a social media marketer do?
They plan content, create or brief creative, publish and engage, run paid campaigns, and report results. In small teams one person often does all of it; larger teams split organic and paid.
### Is social media marketing good for small businesses?
Yes. It lets small brands reach precise audiences on modest budgets and compete on ideas rather than spend. Start narrow and grow what works.
### How much does social media marketing cost?
Organic costs time. Paid costs vary widely by platform and audience: reaching senior B2B roles on LinkedIn usually costs more per click than broad consumer audiences on Meta or TikTok.
### Does social media marketing help SEO?
Not as a direct ranking factor, but it drives branded searches, links and mentions, and those help search visibility and AI answers.
:::
