Email marketing is sending planned messages to people who have agreed to hear from you, to build a relationship, move leads toward a sale and keep customers coming back. It covers newsletters, welcome series, nurture sequences, product updates, offers and re-engagement emails. Unlike social or search, you own the list: no algorithm decides whether your subscribers see you. That's why email marketing remains one of the steadiest channels in B2B.

:::takeaways
- Permission comes first. A smaller list of people who asked to hear from you beats a large bought list every time.
- Deliverability is now a hard requirement: authenticate your domain, keep complaints low and make unsubscribing easy.
- Judge emails on clicks, replies and pipeline. Open rates are inflated by privacy features.
:::

## Why email marketing matters

Email reaches people you've already earned. It nurtures the large share of buyers who aren't ready yet, and it turns one-time customers into repeat ones. It also personalizes well: in the Content Marketing Institute's 2026 research, 89% of B2B marketers said their organization personalizes content, and 85% of those use personalized email ([CMI and MarketingProfs, 2025](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/press-room/new-b2b-research-finds-winning-marketing-teams-are-building-fundamentals-not-just-playing-with-prompts)).

## The sender rules you must meet

Gmail has enforced stricter rules for bulk senders since 2024, and its current guidelines are clear:

:::stats "Gmail's bulk sender rules" highlight=1
5,000+ | messages a day to Gmail accounts makes you a bulk sender | Google Workspace Admin Help, 2026 | https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126
0.30% | spam rate you must stay below (Google advises under 0.10%) | Google Workspace Admin Help, 2026 | https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126
:::

Bulk senders must authenticate with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, and marketing emails must support one-click unsubscribe plus a visible unsubscribe link. Even if you send less, these are good practice. Our [email deliverability checklist](/resources/email-deliverability-checklist/) walks through each one.

:::myth-fact
Myth: A high open rate means the email worked.
Fact: Apple's Mail Privacy Protection downloads remote content in the background when a message arrives, which can register as an "open" ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/mail/protect-email-privacy-mlhlp1205/mac)). Clicks, replies and conversions are more reliable.
:::

## An example from our work

:::quote "Joanna Okedara-Kalu" "Founder & CEO, TechDella"
If a client has no email set up, I don't start with a newsletter. I start with the emails people already expect: a welcome message after signup, a follow-up after someone downloads a guide, and a confirmation after they book a call. Those go to people who just raised their hand. Once they work and the domain is authenticated, a regular newsletter has something solid to build on.
:::

## Which emails to set up first

| Email | Trigger | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome series | New subscriber or lead | Set expectations, deliver what they signed up for |
| Lead nurture | Download, webinar or tool use | Move from interest to a sales conversation |
| Newsletter | Fixed schedule | Stay top of mind with useful ideas |
| Post-purchase or onboarding | New customer | Faster value, fewer cancellations |
| Re-engagement | No clicks in 90 to 180 days | Win back or remove inactive contacts |

Most of these run automatically once set up. See [marketing automation](/glossary/marketing-automation/) for how.

## How to start email marketing

:::steps "Six steps to a healthy program"
1. Choose a platform | Look for automation, segmentation, CRM sync, and support for authentication and one-click unsubscribe.
2. Authenticate your domain | Set SPF, DKIM and DMARC before your first send. Send from your own domain, not a free mailbox.
3. Grow the list with consent | Use forms, lead magnets and events. Unticked consent boxes. Never buy lists.
4. Build the welcome series | Three to five emails that deliver value and introduce what you do.
5. Write for one reader | One idea, one call to action. Test subject lines with our [subject line analyzer](/tools/email-subject-line-analyzer/).
6. Clean and measure monthly | Remove hard bounces and long-inactive contacts. Track clicks, replies and pipeline.
:::

:::faq
### What is a good click rate for email marketing?
It varies by industry and list. Instead of chasing a benchmark, compare against your own last six months and look for steady improvement, especially in clicks from your target accounts.
### How often should I send marketing emails?
Often enough to stay useful, rarely enough that people don't complain. For most B2B newsletters that's every two to four weeks, plus automated sequences.
### Is email marketing legal without consent?
Rules differ by country. The EU and UK generally require consent for marketing email to individuals. The US CAN-SPAM Act requires an opt-out and honest headers. Consent is the safest standard everywhere.
### Is email marketing still effective in 2026?
Yes. It's one of the few channels you fully own, and it works best paired with search, content and automation.
:::
