Omnichannel marketing means connecting all the channels a customer uses (your website, email, ads, social, chat, sales calls and support) so each one knows what happened in the others. A buyer who downloaded a guide shouldn't get an ad for that same guide. A customer who just raised a support ticket shouldn't get an upsell email the same afternoon. Omnichannel marketing makes the experience feel like one conversation instead of many disconnected ones.

:::takeaways
- Multichannel means being on many channels. Omnichannel means those channels share data and context.
- The foundation is a single customer record, usually your CRM, that every channel reads from and writes to.
- Start with two or three connections that fix real friction, not a full rebuild.
:::

## Omnichannel vs multichannel

| | Multichannel | Omnichannel |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | The channel | The customer |
| Data | Separate per tool | Shared customer record |
| Message | Same campaign pushed everywhere | Next step based on what the person did |
| Example | An email blast and the same ad to everyone | A visitor who viewed pricing gets a case study email, then sales follows up |
| Measurement | Per channel | Across the journey |

## Why omnichannel marketing matters

Buyers move between channels constantly. The average online adult uses 6.75 social platforms a month alone ([DataReportal Digital 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-two-in-three-people-use-social-media)), before you count search, email, AI assistants and messaging apps. B2B buying adds several people per deal, each touching different channels. When channels don't talk, buyers repeat themselves, get irrelevant messages and drift away. When they do, follow-ups are timely and relevant, which shortens sales cycles and raises [customer lifetime value](/glossary/customer-lifetime-value/).

:::myth-fact
Myth: Omnichannel is only for big retailers with apps and stores.
Fact: A B2B company with a website, a CRM, an email tool and a sales team can be omnichannel. It just needs those tools to share one record of each contact.
:::

## An example from our work

:::quote "Joanna Okedara-Kalu" "Founder & CEO, TechDella"
Don't buy a new channel to become omnichannel. Connect the ones you already run. The first thing I check is whether the CRM, the email tool and the website forms share one contact record. If a lead who booked a call still gets your cold newsletter sequence, fix that plumbing before you spend another dollar on reach.
:::

## How to start with omnichannel marketing

:::steps "Five steps for a small team"
1. Map the real journey | Interview five recent customers. List every touchpoint from first contact to signing, and where it felt clunky.
2. Pick your source of truth | Make the CRM the single customer record. Connect forms, email, ads audiences and chat to it.
3. Fix the three worst handoffs | Common ones: web form to sales follow-up, trial to onboarding, and closed deal to marketing suppression lists.
4. Trigger, don't blast | Send messages based on behavior: pricing page visits, guide downloads, webinar attendance, inactivity.
5. Measure the journey | Use consistent UTM tagging and [marketing attribution](/glossary/marketing-attribution/) to see how channels work together, not just which got the last click.
:::

:::checklist "Quick omnichannel health check"
- Every form submission reaches the CRM within minutes
- Customers are excluded from acquisition ads
- Sales can see which emails and pages a lead engaged with
- Unsubscribes and consent choices sync across every tool
- Support issues pause promotional emails for that contact
:::

## Privacy is part of the design

Connecting channels means connecting data, so consent has to travel with it. Record what each person agreed to, respect it in every tool, and use Google's consent mode for ads and analytics where it applies. Done well, omnichannel marketing feels helpful. Done carelessly, it feels like being followed.

:::faq
### What is an example of omnichannel marketing?
A prospect reads a guide, then gets a related email the next day. When they visit the pricing page, sales gets an alert and the ads they see switch from awareness to a case study. Once they sign, they're removed from sales ads and start onboarding emails.
### What are the pillars of omnichannel marketing?
A single customer view, connected channels, consistent messaging and journey-level measurement. Consent and data quality sit underneath all four.
### What's another word for omnichannel?
"Connected" or "integrated" marketing are close. Multichannel is related but different: it means being on many channels, not connecting them.
### Do I need expensive software for omnichannel marketing?
No. A CRM with native email and integrations to your ad platforms covers most needs for small and mid-size B2B teams.
:::
