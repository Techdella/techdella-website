Digital marketing is any marketing that happens online: search engines, AI assistants, social platforms, email, websites, and paid ads across all of them. Its job is the same as all marketing (get the right people to know you, trust you and buy), but digital channels let you target precisely, test quickly and measure what each pound, naira or dollar did. Most businesses don't need every channel. They need the few that match how their buyers research and buy.

:::takeaways
- Digital marketing is a system of channels, not a list of tactics. Search, content, ads, email and automation feed each other.
- Start with the channel closest to existing demand (usually search), then add channels that create demand.
- Measure leads, pipeline and cost per customer, not likes or impressions.
:::

## How digital marketing works as a system

A buyer might see your LinkedIn post, search your name a week later, read a guide, download a checklist, get three nurture emails and then book a call. No single channel "did it." That's why the channels should be planned together, with shared tracking, a clear [sales funnel](/glossary/sales-funnel/) and honest [marketing attribution](/glossary/marketing-attribution/).

:::myth-fact
Myth: You need to be on every channel to compete.
Fact: Spreading a small budget across seven channels usually means doing all of them badly. Two channels run well beat seven run thinly.
:::

## How to build a digital marketing plan

:::steps "From goal to first results"
1. Set one business goal | For example: 20 qualified sales calls a month within two quarters.
2. Work backward to numbers | If 1 in 4 calls becomes a client and you want 5 clients a month, you need 20 calls. If 10% of leads book a call, you need 200 leads.
3. Pick channels by intent | Capture existing demand first (search, search ads), then create demand (social, content, reach ads).
4. Set up tracking before spending | GA4 events, UTM links, CRM fields and consent mode. Our [UTM generator](/tools/free-utm-parameter-generator/) keeps links consistent.
5. Launch, measure monthly, cut what doesn't work | Move budget toward channels with the lowest cost per qualified lead.
:::

## Metrics that matter

Track cost per qualified lead, lead-to-customer rate, customer acquisition cost, and revenue per customer. Use traffic and engagement as early signals, not as the goal. Review them together each month so you can see how the [whole system](/services/) is performing.

:::faq
### What are the main types of digital marketing?
Search engine optimization, AI search optimization, paid advertising, content marketing, email marketing, social media marketing and marketing automation. Most strategies combine three or four.
### How much should a small business spend on digital marketing?
It depends on margins and goals. Start with a test budget you can sustain for at least three months, so you have enough data to judge each channel fairly.
### What's the difference between digital marketing and digital advertising?
Digital advertising is the paid part: ads you buy on search, social and display networks. Digital marketing includes unpaid channels too, such as SEO, content, email and organic social.
### Which digital marketing channel works fastest?
Paid search usually brings traffic within days, because it targets people already searching. SEO and content take longer but keep working without paying for each click.
:::
