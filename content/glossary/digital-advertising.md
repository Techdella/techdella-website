Digital advertising is paid promotion delivered online: search ads, social media ads, video ads, display banners, native ads, podcast and streaming audio ads, and ads on connected TV. It's bought in real-time auctions, through direct deals with publishers, or through programmatic platforms that buy across thousands of sites at once. Digital advertising stands out from traditional media because it can be targeted precisely, changed in hours, and measured, though measurement is harder than it used to be.

:::takeaways
- Each format does a different job: search captures demand, social and video create it, display and remarketing remind.
- Buying method matters: self-serve auctions, programmatic and direct deals have different costs and controls.
- Privacy changes mean you need first-party data, consent and modeled measurement, not just pixel counts.
:::

## Digital advertising formats

| Format | Where it runs | Main job | Usually bought by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search ads | Google, Bing, marketplaces | Capture people actively looking | Cost per click |
| Social ads | LinkedIn, Meta, TikTok, X, Snapchat | Reach chosen audiences, generate leads | Impressions or clicks, optimized to results |
| Video ads | YouTube, social, streaming | Build awareness and explain products | Views or impressions |
| Display and native | Websites and apps | Reach and remarketing | Impressions, often programmatic |
| Audio | Podcasts, streaming music | Niche reach with trusted hosts | Impressions or sponsorship |
| Connected TV | Streaming TV apps | TV-style reach with digital targeting | Impressions |
| Retail media | Retailer sites and apps | Influence purchase near checkout | Clicks or impressions |

Most B2B advertisers focus on search and LinkedIn first, then add video and remarketing. Consumer brands lean more on Meta, TikTok, YouTube and retail media.

## How digital ads are bought

- **Self-serve auctions:** you set targeting and bids in Google Ads, LinkedIn Campaign Manager or Meta Ads Manager. Most small and mid-size advertisers buy this way.
- **Programmatic:** software buys impressions across many publishers in real time, through a demand-side platform. Good for scale; needs brand-safety controls.
- **Direct and sponsorships:** you agree a placement with a publisher, newsletter or podcast. Less flexible, often more trusted.

For pricing models and budgeting, see [paid advertising](/glossary/paid-advertising/).

:::myth-fact
Myth: Digital ads can be measured perfectly.
Fact: Consent choices, browser privacy features and cross-device journeys mean platforms now model part of their conversions. Treat platform numbers as estimates and check them against your CRM.
:::

## How to measure digital advertising in 2026

:::steps "A measurement setup you can trust"
1. Track real outcomes | Form fills, calls, purchases and qualified leads, sent to each platform and to GA4.
2. Use consent mode and server-side signals | Google's consent mode, Meta's Conversions API and LinkedIn's Conversions API recover signal while respecting choices.
3. Feed your CRM back | Send offline conversions (qualified lead, closed deal) to the platforms so they optimize for quality.
4. Compare across sources | Platform reports, GA4 and CRM will disagree. Use the CRM as the source of truth for revenue.
5. Test incrementality | Where volume allows, run holdout or geo tests to see what ads add beyond what would have happened anyway.
6. Look past last click | Use [marketing attribution](/glossary/marketing-attribution/) views that show how awareness ads support later conversions.
:::

## Common pitfalls

- Spreading a small budget across too many formats to learn anything.
- Judging awareness campaigns on immediate leads. Their job is [brand awareness](/glossary/brand-awareness/) and future demand.
- Ignoring brand safety and placement reports in programmatic and display.
- Running ads without frequency caps, so the same person sees the same ad dozens of times.

:::faq
### Is digital advertising the same as paid advertising?
Nearly. Paid advertising can include offline media such as print or outdoor. Digital advertising is the online part: search, social, video, display, audio and connected TV.
### How quickly can digital advertising produce results?
Search ads can bring traffic within days. Awareness formats like video and social reach build results over weeks and months.
### Do small businesses need digital advertising?
Not always, but it's the fastest way to test offers and reach new audiences. Start small, on one platform where your buyers are, with clear tracking.
### What is programmatic advertising?
Automated buying of ad impressions across many websites and apps in real time, using software and data to decide which impressions to buy and at what price.
:::
