White label email marketing is a setup where an agency offers email marketing to its clients under its own brand, while a third party supplies the platform, the people or both. The client sees the agency's logo, domain and reports. The provider stays invisible. It lets agencies add email as a service without building software or hiring a full email team.

:::takeaways
- There are two models: white label software (you do the work on a rebranded platform) and white label services (a partner does the work under your name).
- You keep the client relationship, so you also own the results, including deliverability problems.
- Check sender authentication, data ownership and exit terms before you sign.
:::

## The two models

| | White label software | White label service |
|---|---|---|
| Who does the work | Your team | The partner's team |
| What's rebranded | Login, dashboard, reports, domain | Deliverables and reports |
| Best for | Agencies with email skills but no platform | Agencies that want to sell email without delivery staff |
| Main risk | Your team's capacity | Quality control and response times |
| Margin | Higher, more effort | Lower, less effort |

Many agencies mix the two: a rebranded platform plus a partner for strategy, copy or setup.

## Why white label email marketing matters

Email is one of the few channels where you own the audience. For an agency, adding it raises the value of each client and makes results stickier. White label lets you offer it quickly. The catch: mailbox providers now enforce strict rules, and those rules apply to every client account you run.

:::stats "Rules every client account must meet" highlight=1
5,000+ | messages a day to Gmail makes a sender a bulk sender | Google Workspace Admin Help, 2026 | https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126
0.30% | spam rate that bulk senders must stay below (Google advises under 0.10%) | Google Workspace Admin Help, 2026 | https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126
:::

Bulk senders must also authenticate with SPF, DKIM and DMARC and support one-click unsubscribe. If one client on a shared setup ignores these rules, others can suffer. That's why authentication per client domain matters.

If you resell email under your own brand, you own the deliverability risk for every client, so I'd set rules before signing anyone. Each client sends from their own authenticated domain, imports only lists they can show consent for, and gets warmed up gradually. ## How to set up white label email marketing

:::steps "From partner choice to first send"
1. Pick the model | Decide whether your team will run campaigns or a partner will. This drives cost, margin and hiring.
2. Vet the provider | Ask for client references, uptime, support hours and how they handle spam complaints and bounces.
3. Authenticate each client domain | Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC on each client's sending domain, not one shared domain. Our [email deliverability checklist](/resources/email-deliverability-checklist/) covers the steps.
4. Agree data ownership in writing | The client owns their list. Confirm you can export contacts and history at any time.
5. Build reporting clients understand | Report clicks, replies, conversions and revenue. Treat opens as a rough signal only.
6. Write your service levels | Turnaround times, approval steps and who answers when something breaks.
:::

:::myth-fact
Myth: With white label, the provider is responsible if emails land in spam.
Fact: Mailbox providers judge the sending domain and its reputation. Your client sees your brand, so you own the problem and the fix.
:::

## What to check before choosing a platform

- Full rebranding: custom domain for the login, your logo, and reports with no provider marks.
- Sub-accounts per client, with separate sending domains and separate suppression lists.
- Automation: welcome flows, nurture sequences and triggers from your CRM. See [marketing automation](/glossary/marketing-automation/).
- Consent tools: double opt-in, preference centers and one-click unsubscribe.
- Pricing that scales with contacts or sends, and clear exit terms.

:::faq
### Is white label email marketing legal?
Yes. Reselling software or services under your brand is a normal business arrangement. You still have to follow email law in each market, such as CAN-SPAM in the US and GDPR in the EU and UK, including consent and unsubscribe rules.
### How do agencies price white label email marketing?
Most charge a monthly retainer per client that covers platform cost, campaign work and reporting. The margin depends on how much of the work your team does versus the partner.
### Can clients find out who the provider is?
On a fully white-labeled platform, the login, domain and reports carry your brand. Technical traces such as server names can sometimes reveal the provider, so be ready to explain your setup if asked.
### What's the biggest risk?
Deliverability. One poorly managed client list can damage reputation on shared infrastructure. Separate domains and strict list hygiene reduce that risk.
:::
