A digital marketing funnel is a simple map of how people go from never having heard of you to becoming a customer online. It's usually split into three stages: top of funnel (awareness), middle of funnel (consideration) and bottom of funnel (conversion), with retention added after the sale. The model's value is practical: it tells you what content, channel and call to action fit each stage, and which number to watch.

:::takeaways
- Top of funnel earns attention, middle of funnel earns trust, bottom of funnel earns the decision.
- Each stage needs its own content, channel mix, call to action and metric.
- Automation and retargeting connect the stages, so interested people don't drift away.
- Real buyers don't move in a straight line. Use the funnel for planning, not as a prediction.
:::

## The stages at a glance

| Stage | Buyer's question | Content that fits | Channels | Metric to watch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TOFU: awareness | "Why is this happening, and is it a problem?" | Guides, glossary terms, short videos, social posts | Organic search, social, PR | Reach, new visitors, engaged sessions |
| MOFU: consideration | "What are my options, and who does this well?" | Case studies, comparisons, webinars, tools, checklists | Email, retargeting, LinkedIn, search | Leads captured, email engagement, return visits |
| BOFU: conversion | "Is this the right partner, and what will it cost?" | Pricing, proposals, demos, audits, testimonials | Sales calls, branded search, direct | Conversion rate, cost per acquisition |
| Retention | "Did we choose well, and what next?" | Onboarding, results reports, new-feature updates | Email, account management | Retention, expansion, referrals |

The [sales funnel](/glossary/sales-funnel/) picks up from the moment someone becomes a lead and tracks what sales does next.

## Why the digital marketing funnel matters

Without it, teams over-invest in one stage. A common pattern is a blog full of beginner explainers with no case studies, comparisons or pricing guidance behind them. Another is ads that ask cold audiences to "book a demo" on first contact. Mapping content and calls to action by stage exposes both problems.

It's also a better way to judge channels. Organic social may produce few direct leads but large top-of-funnel reach. Judging it by bottom-of-funnel metrics would undervalue it. Judging branded search by reach would miss that it captures demand created elsewhere.

:::myth-fact
Myth: Every piece of content should drive a sale.
Fact: Top-of-funnel content earns attention and trust. Give it a fitting next step, such as a related guide or a newsletter, rather than a sales call.
Myth: The funnel ends at the purchase.
Fact: Retention, upsells and referrals are where much of a B2B customer's value comes from. Include a post-purchase stage in your plan.
:::

## How to build a digital marketing funnel

:::steps "From audit to working funnel"
1. List what you already have | Put every page, email sequence, ad and resource into a spreadsheet and tag it TOFU, MOFU, BOFU or retention.
2. Find the empty stages | Most teams find gaps in the middle: few case studies, comparisons or tools.
3. Give each asset a next step | Every piece needs a [call to action](/glossary/call-to-action-cta/) that moves the reader one stage forward.
4. Connect stages with automation | Use [marketing automation](/glossary/marketing-automation/) to send relevant follow-ups when someone downloads, visits pricing or goes quiet.
5. Retarget engaged visitors | Use [remarketing](/glossary/remarketing/) to show consideration-stage content to people who read top-of-funnel pages.
6. Set one metric per stage | Report each stage on its own metric, then track how many people move from one stage to the next each month.
:::

### Example automation between stages

- **Download a guide (TOFU to MOFU):** send the guide, then two emails with a related case study and a checklist over the next week.
- **Visit the pricing page twice (MOFU to BOFU):** alert the account owner and send a short email offering a call.
- **No activity for 60 days:** move the contact to a monthly newsletter instead of sales follow-up.
- **Customer for 90 days (retention):** send a results summary and ask for a review or referral.

### How AI search affects the top of the funnel

More early research now happens in AI answers, which often satisfy simple questions without a click. Measure the top of the funnel by more than visits: branded search and direct traffic show whether awareness is growing.

## Related terms

- [Call to action (CTA)](/glossary/call-to-action-cta/): the step that moves people between stages.

- [Remarketing](/glossary/remarketing/): bringing engaged visitors back.

:::faq
### What is the difference between a marketing funnel and a sales funnel?
The marketing funnel covers how people discover and consider you, up to becoming a lead. The sales funnel covers what happens after: qualification, proposals and closing. In B2B, they meet at the handoff from marketing to sales, which should have a clear, agreed definition.
### What do TOFU, MOFU and BOFU stand for?
Top of funnel, middle of funnel and bottom of funnel. TOFU content attracts people who are learning about a problem. MOFU content helps them compare solutions. BOFU content helps them decide and act, such as pricing pages, demos and consultations.
### How long should a digital marketing funnel be?
As long as your buyers need. A low-cost product may go from ad to purchase in one visit. A B2B service with several decision-makers can take months, with many touchpoints. Use your CRM data to see how long deals actually take.
### How do I know if my funnel is working?
Look at conversion between stages, not just totals. If plenty of people engage at the top but few become leads, the middle needs work. If leads are plentiful but few buy, look at qualification, proof and pricing.
:::
