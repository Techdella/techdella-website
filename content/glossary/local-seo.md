Local SEO is how a business shows up when someone searches for a service near them: "accountant in Lagos", "IT support near me", or a search typed while standing on a street corner. Most of those results come from the map pack, which is driven by your Google Business Profile, not only your website. Local SEO is the work of making both of them strong.

:::takeaways
- Google says local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and popularity.
- You can't pay for a better local ranking. You can only improve the signals Google reads.
- Your Google Business Profile is the single most important local asset.
- Only build location pages for places where you actually serve customers.
:::

## How Google ranks local results

Google's own help page for Business Profiles names three factors. Knowing which ones you can influence saves a lot of wasted effort.

| Factor | What Google means | How much you control it |
|---|---|---|
| Relevance | How well your profile matches the search | High: categories, services, description, website content |
| Distance | How far you are from the searcher or the place named | Very little: your address is your address |
| Popularity | How well known your business is, online and offline | Medium: reviews, mentions, links, a site that ranks well |

The same page also calls the third factor "prominence" and says it draws on how many websites link to your business and how many reviews you have. More reviews and positive ratings can help. Google is also explicit that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Ads can appear above the pack, but they don't change the organic order.

:::myth-fact
Myth: Stuffing keywords into your business name helps you rank locally.
Fact: It breaks Google's Business Profile guidelines and can get the profile suspended. Use your real-world business name.
Myth: Every city you might serve needs its own landing page.
Fact: Near-identical city pages are what Google's spam policies call doorway abuse. Build a page only where you have real customers, staff or proof.
:::

## Why local SEO matters

For a business that serves a defined area, the map pack is often the first thing a buyer sees. It shows your rating, hours, photos and a call button before anyone visits your site. If your profile is incomplete or your reviews are thin, a competitor with a weaker website can still win the call.

Local SEO also matters for B2B firms with offices: people still search "[service] + [city]" when they want a partner they can meet in person.

## How to do local SEO

:::steps "A local SEO plan you can run in a month"
1. Claim and verify your profile | Claim your Google Business Profile, verify it, and make sure the name, address and phone number match your website exactly.
2. Choose categories carefully | Pick the primary category that best describes your main service. Add secondary categories only if you genuinely offer them.
3. Fill every field | Services, service areas, hours, holiday hours, photos of your real premises and team, and a plain-English description.
4. Ask for reviews consistently | Ask every satisfied customer, at the moment they're happiest. Reply to every review, including the bad ones.
5. Fix your listings | Correct your details on the main directories and industry sites in your country. Consistency matters more than volume.
6. Build one strong page per real location | Include local proof, directions, local FAQs and LocalBusiness structured data only if a staffed address exists.
:::

Keyword choice matters here too. Use [keyword research](/glossary/keyword-research/) to learn whether people in your market search "near me", the city name, or the neighborhood. Mark up real locations with [schema markup](/glossary/schema-markup/) so your address, hours and phone number are machine-readable.

### Local SEO checklist

:::checklist "Before you call it done"
- Name, address and phone match on your profile, website and top directories
- Primary category matches your main service
- At least ten recent photos of your real business
- A review request built into your sales or delivery process
- Each location page has unique content and local proof
- The site works well on a phone, where most local searches happen
:::

:::faq
### How long does local SEO take to work?
Profile fixes such as categories and business details can change visibility within weeks once Google processes them. Review volume and popularity build more slowly, usually over several months. The fastest wins tend to come from fixing wrong categories, mismatched contact details and missing service information.
### Do I need a physical address for local SEO?
To appear in the map pack you need a Business Profile, which requires either a storefront customers can visit or a service-area setup that hides your address. Pure online businesses without a place to meet customers aren't eligible for a Business Profile.
### Do reviews help local rankings?
Google says more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking, so yes. Reviews also affect whether people click. Never buy reviews or offer incentives for positive ones; both break Google's policies and can lead to removal.
### Is local SEO useful for B2B companies?
Yes, when buyers look for a nearby partner, such as IT services, accountants, agencies or manufacturers. It's less useful for B2B firms that sell only remotely. In that case, national SEO and content usually deliver more.
:::
