To start a cleaning business, pick one niche, register it, insure it, buy a basic kit and sell recurring visits rather than one-off cleans. Most owners start alone or with one helper, so startup costs are low. What decides whether the business pays is pricing per job, travel time between jobs and how many clients stay on a weekly or monthly schedule.

This guide shows how to start a cleaning business in the US, the UK and Nigeria: the niches that work, what it costs, how to register, how to price, and how to fill your calendar.

:::takeaways
- Recurring contracts are the business. One-off deep cleans are how you win them.
- Registration and insurance come before your first paid job, in every country.
- Price per job from your real hourly cost, including travel and supplies, not from a competitor's flyer.
- A Google Business Profile with reviews is the cheapest client channel most cleaning businesses have.
:::

## Which cleaning niche should you start with?

Pick one niche for your first six months. Your kit, pricing and marketing all depend on it. A [niche market](/glossary/niche-market/) also makes word of mouth easier: "the person who cleans short-let flats" is easier to recommend than "a cleaner".

| Niche | Who pays | Visit pattern | Kit beyond basics | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Homes (residential) | Busy households | Weekly or fortnightly | Good vacuum, microfiber system | Last-minute cancellations |
| Offices and shops | Office managers, landlords | Daily or weekly, often after hours | Commercial vacuum, floor machine later | Long sales cycles, contract terms |
| Short-let turnovers (Airbnb, serviced flats) | Hosts and property managers | Between every guest | Linen supply, restocking list | Tight time windows |
| End of tenancy / move-out | Tenants, letting agents | One-off, seasonal peaks | Oven and carpet kit | Feast-or-famine demand |
| Post-construction | Builders, developers | One-off per project | HEPA vacuum, PPE | Dust, safety, payment terms |
| Carpets, upholstery, pressure washing | Homes and businesses | Every few months | Extractor or pressure washer | Higher kit cost |

Short-let turnovers and offices are the easiest route to recurring revenue. Homes are the easiest to start, because a single referral can fill a morning.

## How do you register a cleaning business?

Register before your first paid job. It keeps your tax affairs clean and many commercial clients will ask for your registration and insurance documents before they sign.

**United States.** Decide between a sole proprietorship and an LLC, then register with your state and local government as the [SBA explains](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/register-your-business). Get an EIN from the IRS, which is [free and takes minutes online](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number). Licensing is set by your state and city, so check both ([SBA licensing guide](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/apply-licenses-permits)). Many commercial clients also ask for a surety bond.

**United Kingdom.** Most cleaners start as sole traders. You must register for Self Assessment once you earn more than £1,000 from self-employment in a tax year ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-sole-trader)). A limited company costs £100 to register online with [Companies House](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company). The moment you hire anyone, employers' liability insurance is a legal requirement, with a minimum of £5 million cover ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/employers-liability-insurance)).

**Nigeria.** Register a business name or a private limited company with the [Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)](https://www.cac.gov.ng/), then get a tax ID. Commercial clients and estate managers often ask for your CAC certificate before they add you to a vendor list. [SMEDAN](https://smedan.gov.ng/) runs support programs for micro and small businesses. Ask your local government about any trade permit for your area.

## How should you price cleaning jobs?

Start from cost, then check the market. Here is a worked example for a solo home cleaner in the US. The numbers are illustrative, not a forecast.

- Target pay for yourself: $25 an hour.
- Supplies, insurance, phone and fuel: $6 per working hour.
- Unpaid time (travel, quoting, admin): add 25%.
- Hourly cost: ($25 + $6) × 1.25 = **$38.75**.
- A standard two-bedroom clean takes you 3 hours: 3 × $38.75 = **$116.25**, so you might quote $120.

Now the recurring maths. Twelve clients on fortnightly cleans at $120 is 12 × $120 × 26 visits a year = **$37,440** in revenue, before tax. Add four clients and you pass $49,000. That is why the second clean matters more than the first.

Taxes change the take-home figure. In the US, self-employment tax is 15.3% on top of income tax once net earnings reach $400 ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employment-tax-social-security-and-medicare-taxes)). In the UK, sole traders pay Class 4 National Insurance at 6% on profits between £12,570 and £50,270 ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/self-employed-national-insurance-rates)). In Nigeria, under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 the first ₦800,000 of personal taxable income is taxed at 0% and the next ₦2,200,000 at 15% ([Aluko & Oyebode, 2025](https://aluko-oyebode.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Overview-of-the-Notable-Changes-Introduced-by-the-New-Nigeria-Tax-Acts-2025.pdf)). Ask an accountant how this applies to you.

## How do you get cleaning clients?

Clients search for cleaners nearby and pick from the businesses with reviews, clear prices and an easy way to book. Build those three things first.

- **Google Business Profile.** List your service area, services and hours. Add real photos of your work. Ask every client for a review after the second visit.
- **A simple website.** One page per service and per area you serve, with prices or "from" prices and a booking form. Our [local SEO service](/services/local-seo/) covers this in depth, and [TechDella Studios](studios:/services/website-design/) can build the site.
- **Partnerships.** Letting agents, short-let hosts, property managers and builders send repeat work. One property manager can be worth twenty households.
- **Retention.** A client who stays a year is worth far more than a new lead. Track [customer retention](/glossary/customer-retention/) monthly and call anyone who skips two visits.

:::faq
### How much does it cost to start a cleaning business?
A solo home cleaning business can start with a few hundred dollars, pounds or a few hundred thousand naira for a basic kit, products and insurance. Commercial and specialist cleaning cost more because of machines, larger insurance limits and sometimes bonding. Budget for three months of supplies and fuel before recurring income settles.
### Do I need a license to start a cleaning business?
In the US, licensing is set by your state and city, so check both. In the UK, there is no special cleaning licence, but you must register for tax once you earn over £1,000 a year and hold employers' liability insurance if you hire. In Nigeria, register with the CAC and check local government permits.
### Is a cleaning business profitable?
It can be, if most revenue is recurring and your jobs are close together. Profit leaks through travel time, underpriced first quotes and cancellations. Track revenue per hour worked, including travel, every month.
### Should I start with residential or commercial cleaning?
Residential is faster to start because decisions are quick and referrals travel fast. Commercial pays more per contract and is steadier, but needs stronger insurance, references and patience with procurement. Many owners start residential and add offices once they have staff.
### How do I find my first cleaning clients?
Start with people who already trust you: neighbors, former employers, local landlords. Then set up a Google Business Profile, ask for reviews and partner with letting agents or short-let hosts. A clear website with prices and booking turns those searches into jobs.
:::
