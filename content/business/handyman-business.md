To start a handyman business, first learn which jobs you can legally take in your area, then register and insure the business, price every job with a minimum call-out charge, and build repeat work from landlords, property managers and short-let hosts. The legal line matters most. Gas work, much electrical and plumbing work, and jobs above certain values often need a licensed or registered trade.

This guide covers how to start a handyman business in the US, the UK and Nigeria, with licensing limits, startup costs, pricing maths and how to keep your diary full.

:::takeaways
- Know your legal limits. Unlicensed gas, electrical or large-value work can bring fines and void insurance.
- Public liability insurance is the first thing to buy. Many landlords and agents will not book you without it.
- A minimum charge stops small jobs from losing money once travel is counted.
- Property managers and landlords give repeat work. Households give referrals.
:::

## What jobs can a handyman legally do?

This is where most new handyman businesses get into trouble. Rules vary by country, state and city, so check locally before you advertise a service.

**United States.** Licensing is set by states and cities. The Bureau of Labor Statistics notes that basic work usually needs no licence, but electrical or plumbing work may, and requirements vary by state and locality ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/general-maintenance-and-repair-workers.htm)). For example, California's minor work exemption lets unlicensed people take jobs under $1,000 in combined labor and materials, only where no building permit is needed and no employees are used; jobs can't be split to fit under the limit ([CSLB, 2024](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/Resources/IndustryBulletins/2024/AB%202622%20Implementation.FINAL.pdf)).

**United Kingdom.** Registering with Gas Safe Register is a legal requirement for anyone carrying out gas work ([UK regulated professions register](https://www.regulated-professions.service.gov.uk/professions/registered-gas-engineer)). Some electrical work, such as replacing a fuse box or changing electrics near a bath or shower, may need building regulations approval unless you are registered with a competent person scheme ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/building-regulations-approval/when-you-need-approval)).

**Nigeria.** There is no single national handyman licence. Register with the [CAC](https://www.cac.gov.ng/), and leave electrical installations and gas work to certified professionals. Estate managers and facility companies often require a registered business and references before adding you as a vendor.

| Usually fine for a handyman | Often needs a licensed or registered trade |
|---|---|
| Hanging shelves, TVs, curtains, pictures | Gas appliances, boilers, gas pipework |
| Flat-pack assembly | New circuits, fuse boards, wiring near water |
| Minor drywall, plaster and paint repairs | New plumbing runs, water heaters |
| Door, lock, hinge and cabinet fixes | Structural changes, load-bearing walls |
| Caulking, sealing, tiling repairs | Any job needing a building permit |
| Gutter clearing, fence and deck repairs | Jobs over local value limits (US states) |

## How should a handyman price jobs?

Use an hourly rate to build job prices, and always charge a minimum. Worked example (illustrative), UK handyman:

- Target take-home before tax: £32,000 a year.
- Business costs (van, fuel, insurance, tools, phone): £9,000.
- Revenue needed: £32,000 + £9,000 = **£41,000**.
- Billable hours: 46 weeks × 28 hours = **1,288 hours** (travel, quoting and admin fill the rest).
- Minimum hourly rate: £41,000 ÷ 1,288 = **£31.83**, so charge at least £32 an hour.

Now the minimum charge. A 30-minute job with 40 minutes of travel uses 70 minutes of your day. At £32 an hour that is £37.33, so set a **minimum call-out of £40** that includes the first hour. Materials are charged on top, with a small handling markup.

Many handymen also sell fixed-price tasks: TV mounting, flat-pack assembly per item, or "half day of small jobs" bundles. Bundles fill your day efficiently and make buying easy.

The method works in dollars and naira too. In Nigeria, factor in transport, generator or fuel costs for power tools, and materials price changes; quote materials separately with a validity date.

:::myth-fact
Myth: A handyman can take any small job.
Fact: Job size doesn't decide legality. Gas work in the UK always needs a Gas Safe registered engineer, and many US states license electrical and plumbing work regardless of size.
Myth: Charging by the hour is always fairest.
Fact: Fixed prices for common tasks are easier for clients to say yes to and reward you for getting faster. Use your hourly rate to set them.
:::

:::faq
### Do I need a license to be a handyman?
It depends on where you work and what you do. In the US, states and cities set the rules, and many require licences for electrical, plumbing or larger jobs; California allows unlicensed jobs under $1,000 only without permits or employees. In the UK, gas work requires Gas Safe registration. In Nigeria, register with the CAC and leave gas and electrical installation to certified trades.
### How much does it cost to start a handyman business?
If you own tools and a vehicle, you can start with insurance, registration and basic marketing for a few hundred dollars or pounds. Buying tools and a used van can raise that to several thousand.
### How much should I charge as a handyman?
Calculate your hourly rate from your income target, costs and realistic billable hours, then set a minimum call-out charge that covers travel. Offer fixed prices for common tasks.
### Is a handyman business profitable?
Yes, when jobs are close together, minimums are enforced and you have repeat clients. Travel time and underpriced small jobs are the main profit leaks.
### How do I get my first handyman clients?
Tell your network, set up a Google Business Profile, join local groups, and approach landlords and letting agents with a maintenance offer. Reviews from your first ten jobs matter a lot.
:::
