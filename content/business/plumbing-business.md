To start a plumbing business, you need three things in place before your first paid job: the right licence or registration for where you work, a registered and insured business, and a price list built from your real costs. After that, success comes down to answering the phone fast and showing up on local search when someone's pipe bursts.

This guide walks through how to start a plumbing business in the US, the UK and Nigeria, with official licensing links, a startup-cost table and simple maths for pricing.

:::takeaways
- Licensing is local. US states license plumbers; UK gas work legally requires Gas Safe registration.
- Your van, tools and insurance are the big costs. Start with a used van and buy specialist tools per job.
- Price from your true cost per billable hour, not from what the plumber down the road charges.
- Emergency calls are won by whoever answers first and ranks in the local map results.
:::

## What licence do you need to start a plumbing business?

This is the step you can't skip. Working unlicensed can void your insurance and, for gas work, put lives at risk.

| | United States | United Kingdom | Nigeria |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trade licence | Most states and some localities license plumbers, usually after 2 to 5 years of experience and an exam ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/plumbers-pipefitters-and-steamfitters.htm)). Some states need a separate gas licence. Many require a master or contractor licence to run a business. | No general plumbing licence, but any business doing gas work must be on the Gas Safe Register by law ([HSE](https://www.hse.gov.uk/gas/domestic/faqs.htm)). Unvented hot water and some notifiable work need extra competence schemes. | No single national plumbing licence we could verify. Clients, estates and facility managers ask for proof of training, a registered business and references. Check your state's rules for building and public works. |
| Business registration | State filing for an LLC or corporation; free EIN from the [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number) | Sole trader (Self Assessment above £1,000 trading income) or a £100 limited company via [Companies House](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company) | Business name or company with the [CAC](https://www.cac.gov.ng/), plus a TIN |
| Other permits | Local business licence and permits per job; see the [SBA licensing guide](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/apply-licenses-permits) | VAT once turnover passes £90,000 ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration/when-to-register)) | Local government trade permits and signage fees vary by area |
| Insurance | General liability, commercial auto, workers' comp once you hire | Public liability, van insurance, employers' liability once you hire (a legal requirement) | Public liability and vehicle insurance; check contract requirements |

:::callout warning "Gas work is a legal line"
In Great Britain, a business must be on the Gas Safe Register to carry out gas work covered by the Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998, and it's an offence to falsely claim registration. If you're not registered, refer gas jobs to a registered engineer.
:::

## How much does it cost to start a plumbing business?

These are planning estimates for a one-van operation. Prices vary by area and by whether you buy new or used, so get three quotes for anything over a few hundred.

| Cost item | Planning estimate (US / UK) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Licensing and registration | $100–$1,000 / £100–£500 | State exam and licence fees vary; UK company £100; Gas Safe registration has its own fees |
| Used van with racking | $8,000–$25,000 / £6,000–£18,000 | Lease if cash is tight; the van is also your billboard |
| Core hand and power tools | $3,000–$8,000 / £2,500–£6,000 | Pipe cutters, press tool, drain auger, inspection camera later |
| Starter parts stock | $1,000–$3,000 / £800–£2,500 | Fittings, valves and seals for the jobs you do most |
| Insurance (first year) | $1,500–$5,000 / £800–£3,000 | Depends heavily on cover, claims history and area |
| Website, branding, signage | $500–$3,000 / £400–£2,500 | Van wrap is often your best ad |
| Job management software | Monthly subscription | Quoting, scheduling and invoicing in one place |

In Nigeria, the same items apply, but budget separately for fuel, a phone line customers can reach on WhatsApp, and stock bought in bulk from trusted suppliers to avoid counterfeit fittings. Get quotes in naira on the day you buy, as imported tools track the exchange rate.

## How to price plumbing jobs (with the maths)

The most common mistake is copying a competitor's call-out fee without knowing your own costs.

**Worked example (replace with your numbers):**

- Yearly costs: van $6,000, insurance $3,000, tools and replacement $2,000, software and phone $1,200, marketing $3,000. Total: **$15,200**.
- Your target pay: **$65,000**.
- So the business must earn **$80,200** before materials.
- You work 46 weeks, and about 25 of your 40 weekly hours are billable (the rest is driving, quoting and admin). That's 1,150 billable hours.
- $80,200 ÷ 1,150 = **about $70 per billable hour**, plus materials at cost plus a markup.

If you charge $55 an hour because "that's the going rate", you're working for less than your target before a single slow week. Most plumbing businesses also use a minimum call-out charge that covers the first hour and travel.

:::myth-fact
Myth: Customers always pick the cheapest plumber.
Fact: For emergencies, people pick whoever answers, arrives soon and looks trustworthy. Response time and reviews usually beat price.
Myth: You need a big shop and a crew to start.
Fact: Plenty of plumbing businesses start as one person and a van. Add an apprentice when you're turning down work every week.
:::

## Write a one-page plumbing business plan

Skip the 40-page document. Answer these on one page and revisit them every quarter:

1. **Services:** emergency repairs, bathroom installs, water heaters, drain clearing, new builds. Pick two to lead with.
2. **Customers:** homeowners, landlords and letting agents, property managers, builders. Landlords and agents give repeat work.
3. **Area:** a radius you can reach in 30 minutes. Travel time is unpaid time.
4. **Prices:** your hourly rate, call-out fee and fixed prices for common jobs.
5. **Lead sources:** referrals, local search, trade partners, agent and landlord accounts.
6. **Numbers:** monthly costs, break-even jobs per week and cash buffer.

:::steps "Your first 90 days"
1. Licence and insure | Confirm your licence or Gas Safe status, register the business and get insurance before quoting.
2. Set up the basics | Business bank account, job software, a phone number you'll actually answer, and a quote template.
3. Get found locally | Create a Google Business Profile with your service area, photos and hours, and ask every customer for a review.
4. Build trade partnerships | Introduce yourself to letting agents, property managers, kitchen fitters and builders who need a reliable plumber.
5. Launch a simple website | One page per core service, your area, prices or "from" prices, and a click-to-call button.
6. Review the numbers | At day 90, compare jobs per week and average job value to your break-even.
:::

## Plumbing business FAQs

:::faq
### Can you start a plumbing business without a license?
In most US states, no: you need a state or local plumbing licence, and often a contractor licence to run the business. In the UK there's no general plumbing licence, but gas work legally requires Gas Safe registration. In Nigeria, there's no single national licence, but clients expect proof of training.
### How much does it cost to start a plumbing business?
A one-van operation typically needs a used van, core tools, starter stock, insurance and registration. Our planning estimate is roughly $14,000–$45,000 in the US or £10,500–£32,500 in the UK, depending mostly on the van and whether tools are new.
### How much should I charge per hour as a new plumber?
Add up your yearly business costs and target pay, then divide by realistic billable hours (often 25 of 40 weekly hours). Your rate must cover travel, admin and slow weeks, so it will be well above an employee's hourly wage.
### What insurance does a plumbing business need?
Public or general liability is the base. Add commercial vehicle cover, tools cover, and employers' liability or workers' compensation once you hire. Some contracts require professional indemnity too.
### How do plumbers get customers fast?
Answer every call, collect reviews from every job, and complete your Google Business Profile. Partner with letting agents, landlords and builders for repeat work. Paid search works once your site converts callers.
:::
