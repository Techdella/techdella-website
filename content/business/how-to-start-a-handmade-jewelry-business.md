To start a handmade jewelry business, pick a style and customer you can serve consistently, source materials you can describe truthfully, register the business, and price each piece from materials, labour and overheads with enough margin for shops to stock you. If you work in gold, silver, platinum or palladium, learn the hallmarking and metal-claim rules before you list anything.

This guide covers how to start a handmade jewelry business in the US, the UK and Nigeria, with the legal basics, a cost table and a pricing formula that works for direct and wholesale sales.

:::takeaways
- A clear style (minimalist silver, beaded statement pieces, bridal, men's) is easier to market than a mixed collection.
- In the UK, precious-metal items above set weights must be hallmarked before they're described as gold, silver, platinum or palladium.
- In the US, the FTC's Jewelry Guides explain how to describe metals and stones without misleading buyers.
- Price for wholesale from day one, even if you only sell direct.
:::

## Is handmade jewelry a good business?

Jewelry making is a small, skilled trade with a high share of independents. That's encouraging for makers who can sell well.

-3% | projected change in those jobs, 2025–35 | US Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2025 | https://www.bls.gov/ooh/production/jewelers-and-precious-stone-and-metal-workers.htm

Fewer traditional jewelry jobs doesn't mean less demand for handmade pieces. It reflects factory production and retail changes. Independent makers compete on design, story and personal service, which mass production can't easily copy.

## Step 1: Define your style and customer

Choose one lane to start:

| Style | Typical materials | Price point | Where it sells |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimalist everyday | Sterling silver, gold-filled, small stones | Mid | Online, gift shops |
| Statement and fashion | Brass, beads, resin, mixed media | Low to mid | Markets, Instagram, TikTok |
| Cultural and heritage | Coral and glass beads, brass, cowries | Mid to high | Weddings, events, diaspora customers |
| Bridal and fine | Gold, platinum, diamonds or lab-grown stones | High | Appointments, referrals |
| Men's | Silver, steel, leather, beads | Mid | Online, gifting seasons |

In Nigeria, beadwork and accessories for weddings and celebrations are a familiar market, and diaspora customers buying for events abroad are a natural online audience.

## Step 2: Know the metal and claim rules

| | United States | United Kingdom | Nigeria |
|---|---|---|---|
| Describing metals and stones | The FTC's Guides for the Jewelry, Precious Metals, and Pewter Industries ([16 CFR Part 23](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-23)) explain how to describe gold content, plating, diamonds and lab-created stones without deceiving buyers | Hallmarking is a legal requirement for articles described as gold, silver, platinum or palladium, subject to weight exemptions ([UK Hallmarking guidance](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/63a333b9d3bf7f37654767e0/hallmarking-guidance-notes.pdf)) | No hallmarking scheme comparable to the UK's that we could verify. Describe metals honestly and keep supplier invoices. Locally manufactured products fall under the [Standards Organisation of Nigeria](https://son.gov.ng/mancapservice/). |
| Children's jewelry | Lead and other limits apply to children's products under CPSC rules | Product safety and nickel-release rules apply | General product safety applies |
| Business registration | LLC or sole proprietorship; free EIN ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number)); sales tax permit | Sole trader (Self Assessment once trading income tops £1,000) or £100 limited company ([Companies House](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company)) | Business name or company with the [CAC](https://www.cac.gov.ng/); TIN |

Gold | 1

:::callout warning "Selling to Ireland or the EU?"
UK exemption weights may not apply abroad. The UK government's guidance notes that the Republic of Ireland has no exemption weights, so stock sold there needs suitable marks. Check before you ship precious-metal pieces overseas.
:::

## Step 3: Budget your startup costs

These are planning estimates for a home studio, not quotes. Fine jewelry with gold and stones needs far more material capital.

| Cost item | Planning estimate (US / UK) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hand tools (pliers, cutters, files, mandrels) | $150–$600 / £120–£500 | Quality pliers last years |
| Soldering setup and safety kit | $200–$800 / £150–£600 | Ventilation, fire-safe surface, eye protection |
| Starter materials | $200–$2,000 / £150–£1,500 | Silver and gold prices move daily |
| Packaging and branding | $100–$500 / £80–£400 | Boxes, cards, care instructions |
| Photography setup | $100–$400 / £80–£300 | Light box and a phone camera work well |
| Online shop and domain | $100–$600 a year / £80–£500 a year | Marketplace fees or your own store |
| Hallmarking (UK, precious metals) | Per-item assay office fees | Budget before you price pieces |
| Insurance | $200–$800 / £150–£600 a year | Stock, tools and product liability |

In Nigeria, source beads, wire and findings from established markets and verified suppliers, and expect imported findings and silver prices to move with the exchange rate. Budget for secure storage if you hold precious metals.

## Step 4: Price with a formula

Handmade jewelry is often underpriced because makers forget their time and wholesale.

**Worked example: a sterling silver pendant (replace with your numbers):**

- Materials (silver, chain, jump rings): $14
- Packaging: $2
- Labour: 1.5 hours at $25 = $37.50
- Overheads (tools, website, insurance) per piece: $4
- **Cost: $57.50**
- Wholesale price (cost × 1.5, rounded): **$86**
- Retail price (wholesale × 2): **$172**

If $172 is above what your customers will pay, change the design (thinner chain, simpler setting, faster method) rather than the formula. Selling only direct at $120 still pays you, but you'll never be able to sell through shops at that price.

:::myth-fact
Myth: "Gold" is fine for anything gold-coloured.
Fact: Calling plated or gold-coloured metal "gold" misleads buyers. Use accurate terms like gold-plated or gold-filled, follow the FTC's guides in the US, and hallmark where UK law requires it.
Myth: Lower prices sell more handmade jewelry.
Fact: Handmade buyers pay for design and story. Underpricing signals low quality and leaves no room for wholesale or promotions.
:::

## Step 5: Brand and photograph your work

Jewelry sells on photos. Shoot on a plain background, show scale (on a hand, neck or ear), and include close-ups of clasps and finishing. Encourage customers to share photos wearing your pieces; [user-generated content](/glossary/user-generated-content/) is persuasive social proof.

## Step 6: Sell online and offline

Most handmade jewelry brands mix channels: an online shop for steady sales, markets and events for discovery, wholesale for volume, and custom or bridal commissions for higher-value work. Short videos of pieces being made perform well on social media, and small [paid ad](/services/paid-ads/) tests can show which designs to scale before a holiday season.

## Handmade jewelry business FAQs

:::faq
### How much does it cost to start a handmade jewelry business?
A home setup with tools, a soldering kit, starter materials, packaging, photography and an online shop can start from a few hundred dollars or pounds. Our planning estimate is roughly $1,050–$5,700 in the US, excluding hallmarking fees and any gold or gemstone stock.
### Do I need to hallmark my jewelry in the UK?
If you describe an item as gold, silver, platinum or palladium and it's over the exemption weight (1 g gold, 7.78 g silver, 0.5 g platinum, 1 g palladium), yes. Items below those weights are exempt, with special rules for mixed metals.
### Where can I sell my handmade jewelry?
Your own website, a marketplace, craft markets, pop-ups, boutiques on wholesale terms, and custom commissions. Most successful makers combine an online shop with in-person events.
### Do I need experience to start a jewelry business?
You need enough skill to make consistent, durable pieces. Beading and wirework are easier to start with; soldering and stone setting take training. Take a short course and practise before you sell.
### How should I price handmade jewelry?
Add materials, packaging, labour at a fair hourly rate and a share of overheads to get cost. Multiply by about 1.5 to 2 for wholesale and double that for retail. Adjust the design if the price doesn't fit your market.
:::
