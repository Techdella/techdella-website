To start a coaching business, choose a niche where you can promise a specific outcome, register the business, package your coaching into programs rather than single sessions, and build one dependable way to book discovery calls. Coaching is unregulated in most places, so trust comes from clarity and proof, not from a licence.

This guide walks through how to start a coaching business in the US, the UK and Nigeria, including what it costs, how to price packages, and how to find clients once friends and former colleagues have booked.

:::takeaways
- "Life coach" is not a niche. "Coaching first-time managers in fintech through their first 90 days" is.
- Coaching is not a licensed profession in the US, UK or Nigeria, but you still need to register for tax.
- Sell three-to-six-month packages. Single sessions make income unpredictable and results harder to show.
- Your pipeline is a system: content that attracts, a page that explains, a calendar link that books, and emails that follow up.
:::

## How do you choose a coaching niche?

A good niche answers three questions in one sentence: who you help, what changes for them, and in roughly how long. That sentence is your [value proposition](/glossary/value-proposition/).

| Niche type | Example | Who pays | Typical buying trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Career coaching | New managers, career changers | The individual, sometimes the employer | Promotion, layoff, new role |
| Business coaching | Founders of small service businesses | The business owner | Stalled revenue, hiring the first team |
| Executive coaching | Senior leaders | The employer (HR or L&D budget) | Leadership transition |
| Health and wellness | Busy professionals, new parents | The individual | A health scare, a life change |
| Skills coaching | Public speaking, interviews, sales | Individual or employer | A specific upcoming event |

Pick the niche where you already have credibility. A former HR manager coaching job seekers will close more calls in month one than a career changer coaching founders. ## Do you need a certification to be a coach?

No law in the US, UK or Nigeria requires a coaching certification. A recognized credential, such as one from ICF, helps when you sell to employers whose procurement teams ask for it. For individual clients, a clear niche, testimonials and a structured program matter more. If your coaching touches regulated areas (therapy, financial advice, medical or nutrition claims), stay inside what you are qualified to do, or partner with someone who is.

## How do you register a coaching business?

**United States.** Most coaches start as sole proprietors or form a single-member LLC with their state ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/register-your-business)). Get a [free EIN from the IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number) so you don't hand clients your Social Security number. Check your city for a general business license.

**United Kingdom.** Register as a sole trader for Self Assessment once you earn more than £1,000 from self-employment in a tax year ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-sole-trader)), or set up a limited company for £100 online ([Companies House](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company)). Because you hold client personal data, check whether you need to pay the [ICO data protection fee](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/), which applies to sole traders unless exempt.

**Nigeria.** Register a business name with the [CAC](https://www.cac.gov.ng/) to open a business account and invoice companies. If you coach clients abroad, set up a way to receive foreign payments before your first international client signs.

## How should you price coaching packages?

Price the outcome, then check that the hours make sense. Here is a worked example with illustrative numbers.

- A 12-week program: 6 one-hour calls, a kickoff assessment, and message support between calls.
- Your delivery time: about 6 hours of calls + 4 hours of prep and messages = **10 hours**.
- Price: $1,800. Effective rate: $1,800 ÷ 10 = **$180 an hour**.

Now the annual view. If you can sell two programs a month, that is 2 × 12 × $1,800 = **$43,200** a year, using about 240 delivery hours. Doubling your client count is easier with a group cohort: 8 people at $600 each for the same 12 weeks is $4,800 for roughly the same delivery time.

In Nigeria, many coaches price in naira for local clients and in dollars or pounds for clients abroad. Keep both price lists on separate pages so local buyers are not put off by foreign prices.

Plan for tax before you spend. US sole proprietors pay 15.3% self-employment tax on top of income tax ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employment-tax-social-security-and-medicare-taxes)). UK sole traders pay income tax above the £12,570 Personal Allowance ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/income-tax-rates)) plus Class 4 National Insurance. In Nigeria, personal income tax under the 2025 Act starts at 0% on the first ₦800,000 ([Aluko & Oyebode, 2025](https://aluko-oyebode.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Overview-of-the-Notable-Changes-Introduced-by-the-New-Nigeria-Tax-Acts-2025.pdf)).

## How to start a coaching business: the launch plan

:::steps "Your first 60 days"
1. Write your one-sentence niche | Who you help, what changes, how long. Test it on five people in that niche.
2. Run five free or low-cost pilot clients | Use them to refine the program and earn testimonials you can quote word for word.
3. Register and open a business account | Separate business money from day one. It makes tax time simple.
4. Package the offer | One signature program, one group option, one entry offer (a paid strategy session).
5. Build a booking page | One page: the problem, the outcome, the program, testimonials, price or "from" price, a calendar link.
6. Pick one channel for 90 days | LinkedIn for career and business coaching, Instagram or TikTok for wellness, referrals and communities everywhere.
7. Follow up by email | Every discovery call that says "not yet" goes into a nurture sequence.
:::

:::faq
### How much money do I need to start a coaching business?
You can start for little more than a laptop, a microphone and a booking tool, often under $500 or £400. Training, a custom website and insurance raise that. Spend on whatever gets you in front of buyers first, usually a clear booking page.
### How do I start a coaching business with no experience?
Coach what you already know from work or life, run pilot clients at a low price in exchange for detailed feedback and testimonials, and add a recognized training program once you know the niche suits you.
### Is online coaching profitable?
It can be, because delivery costs are low. Profit depends on filling your calendar. Coaches who sell multi-week programs and keep a nurture list usually have steadier income than those who sell single sessions.
### Should I offer group coaching or one-to-one?
Start one-to-one to learn what clients need. Add a group program once you have repeatable content. Groups lower the price per person and raise your income per hour.
### Do I need insurance as a coach?
It is not required by law for a solo coach, but professional indemnity insurance is common, and corporate clients may ask for it. If you hire staff in the UK, employers' liability insurance is mandatory.
:::
