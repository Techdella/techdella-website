To start a daycare business at home, begin with the rules, not the toys. Home-based childcare is regulated almost everywhere: US states license it, England requires childminders caring for young children to register with Ofsted, and in Nigeria crèches are registered at state level. The rules set how many children you can care for, what training you need and how your home must be set up.

Once you know the rules, the business side is simple: make your space safe, set fees that cover your real costs, and fill your places through parents who trust you.

:::takeaways
- Licensing comes first. It decides how many children you can take, and so your maximum income.
- In England, a childminder can care for up to six children under eight at once, and normally no more than three young children.
- Price from your costs and local rates, and charge for the place, not just the hours a child attends.
- Most families find home daycare through word of mouth and local search.
:::

## What a home daycare business is

A home daycare (called childminding in the UK) is paid care for other people's children in your own home. It's usually smaller and more flexible than a daycare center, and many parents choose it for younger children because of the home setting and the consistent carer.

You might offer:

- Full-day care for babies and toddlers
- Before- and after-school care
- Holiday care for school-age children
- Part-time or flexible places for shift workers

## Licensing rules for home daycare in the US, UK and Nigeria

**United States.** Licensing is set by each state. BLS notes that many states require childcare centers, including those in private homes, to be licensed, often with background checks, immunization records, minimum training and sometimes CPR and first aid certification ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/personal-care-and-service/childcare-workers.htm)). Start with your state's child care licensing agency. [Childcare.gov](https://childcare.gov/consumer-education/regulated-child-care) explains how regulation works and links to each state.

**United Kingdom (England).** Register with Ofsted as a childminder. The Early Years Register fee for childminders is £35, paid every year ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/childminders-and-childcare-providers-register-with-ofsted/fees)). Under the EYFS framework, a childminder may care for a maximum of six children under eight at any one time, including their own, and generally no more than three of them may be young children ([EYFS framework](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/early-years-foundation-stage-framework--2)). Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland have their own regulators.

**Nigeria.** Crèches and daycare centers are registered and inspected at state level. Contact your state ministry responsible for social development or education to confirm what applies to a home-based setting, including space, staff ratios and health requirements. Register the business name with CAC too, so you can open a business account and issue receipts.

## How to start a daycare business at home, step by step

:::steps
1. Confirm the rules | Contact your state licensing office, Ofsted, or your Nigerian state ministry. Write down child limits, training, and inspection steps.
2. Get trained | Pediatric first aid, safeguarding and food hygiene are common requirements and parents ask about them.
3. Prepare your home | Separate play and nap areas, safe sleep setup, gates, outlet covers, secure storage for medicines and cleaning products, and a fire plan.
4. Apply and pass inspection | Submit your application, background checks and any home inspection.
5. Insure the business | Public liability insurance at minimum. Tell your home insurer you're running a business.
6. Write your policies | Hours, fees, sickness, late pick-up, holidays, safeguarding and emergency contacts. Put them in a parent contract.
7. Set your fees | Use the worked example below and local rates.
8. Fill your first places | Tell your network, list with local directories and set up a Google Business Profile.
9. Keep records | Attendance, incidents, medication and payments. Inspectors and parents will ask.
:::

## Fee maths: what a full home daycare can earn

Here's a worked example with illustrative numbers. Use your own local rates and the child limits that apply to you.

- You're allowed four paying children full time (your own child takes another place).
- Weekly fee per child: 200
- Weekly income: 4 × 200 = **800**
- Weekly costs: food 60, materials and cleaning 25, insurance and fees 15, extra utilities 20 = **120**
- Weekly profit before tax: 800 − 120 = **680**
- Over 48 working weeks: 680 × 48 = **32,640**

Now the risk: if one place sits empty for eight weeks, you lose 200 × 8 = 1,600. That's why many providers charge a deposit, charge for booked places rather than attendance, and keep a waiting list.

## How to find families for your daycare

- **Word of mouth.** Tell your neighbors, faith community, school-gate parents and local groups. Give current parents a referral thank-you.
- **Local search.** Parents search "childminder near me" or "daycare in [area]." A complete Google Business Profile with photos, hours and reviews matters. Our [local SEO](/services/local-seo/) team covers what moves the needle, and our glossary explains [local SEO](/glossary/local-seo/) in plain terms.
- **Show your registration.** Parents check Ofsted reports and state license lookups. Make your registration number easy to find.
- **Open days.** Let parents see the space, meet you, and ask questions.

Keeping families is as important as finding them. Clear communication, daily updates and reliable hours drive [customer retention](/glossary/customer-retention/) in childcare more than anything else.

:::faq
### Can I start a daycare at home without experience?
You can, but you'll need the training your regulator requires, and parents will expect it. Many providers start after working in a nursery or school, or after caring for their own children, then add first aid, safeguarding and early-years courses.
### How many children can I look after in a home daycare?
It depends on where you live. In England, a childminder may care for up to six children under eight at once, generally with no more than three young children. US states set their own limits for family child care homes. In Nigeria, check with your state ministry.
### How much should I charge for home daycare?
Check what local providers charge for similar hours and ages, then make sure your fee covers food, materials, insurance, fees and a fair wage for you. Charge for booked places, take a deposit, and review fees once a year.
### Do I need insurance for a home daycare?
Yes. Public liability insurance is the minimum, and many regulators or local authorities expect it. Tell your home insurer you run a business from home, because a standard policy may not cover it.
### How do I get my first daycare clients?
Start with families you already know, then build a Google Business Profile, list with local directories and hold an open day. Reviews from your first families will drive most new inquiries after that.
:::
