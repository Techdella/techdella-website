To start an e-learning business, choose a skill people already pay to learn, prove demand by pre-selling a small pilot, then build the course around the result students want. Pick a platform that works on the devices your learners use, price around the outcome rather than the hours of video, and set up email so you can sell the next cohort without starting from scratch.

This guide covers how to start an e-learning business in Nigeria, the US and the UK, from validation to the consumer rules that apply to digital products.

:::takeaways
- Pre-sell before you record. Ten paying pilot students beat a hundred "I'd buy that" comments.
- Design for your learners' reality: in Nigeria, that often means mobile-first, low-data lessons.
- In the UK, online buyers have a 14-day right to cancel digital content unless they expressly waive it when access starts.
- Courses sell through email and trust, so start building a list before launch day.
:::

What this means for your course: UK and US audiences are almost all online, so you compete on quality and trust. In Nigeria, the online audience is over 100 million people, but data costs and device limits shape how they learn. Short videos, downloadable notes, audio versions and WhatsApp or Telegram communities often work better than long HD lectures.

## Step 1: Choose a topic people pay for

The best course topics sit at the overlap of three things: a skill you've used for real, a result people want badly, and buyers who already spend money on it. Good signs:

- People ask you how to do it, repeatedly.
- There are paid courses, books or coaching on the topic (competition proves demand).
- The result is concrete: a job, a certification pass, a first client, a working spreadsheet, a launched store.

Write the promise in one line: "In [time], you'll be able to [result] without [common obstacle]."

## Step 2: Validate with a paid pilot

:::steps "Pre-sell in three weeks"
1. Write a one-page offer | Who it's for, the result, the outline, the start date, the price.
2. Build a simple landing page | One page with the offer and a payment or waitlist button. See our guide to [landing pages](/glossary/landing-page/).
3. Share it with your network | Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp groups and communities where your learners gather.
4. Set a minimum | Decide in advance: for example, 10 paid students or you refund and rethink.
5. Teach the pilot live | Run it as a small cohort on video calls and record the sessions.
6. Turn the pilot into the course | Edit recordings, add worksheets and fix what confused students.
:::

Live pilots validate demand, give you testimonials, and show exactly where learners get stuck, which is what makes the self-paced version good.

## Step 3: Pick a delivery model and platform

| Model | How it works | Price potential | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-paced course | Pre-recorded lessons, quizzes, downloads | Low to mid | High upfront, low ongoing |
| Cohort course | Fixed start date, live sessions, community | Mid to high | Ongoing per cohort |
| Membership | Monthly fee for new lessons and community | Recurring | Ongoing content |
| Corporate training | Licensed course or live workshops for teams | High | Sales-heavy |

Choose a platform for your learners, not your preferences. Check mobile app quality, offline downloads, local payment options (cards, bank transfer and local gateways in Nigeria), and whether you can export your student list. Your own website plus a course platform gives you more control over search traffic and your brand than a marketplace alone.

## Step 4: Register and follow the rules

| | United States | United Kingdom | Nigeria |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business registration | LLC or sole proprietorship; free EIN ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number)) | Sole trader (Self Assessment once trading income tops £1,000) or £100 limited company ([Companies House](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company)) | Business name or company with the [CAC](https://www.cac.gov.ng/); TIN |
| Consumer rules for digital products | Be careful with earnings or job claims; the FTC treats unsupported claims as deceptive. State sales tax rules on digital products vary. | Buyers can cancel digital content within 14 days unless they expressly request immediate access and acknowledge they lose that right ([Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134)). VAT applies above £90,000 turnover. | Clear refund terms in writing; consumer protection law applies to online sales |
| Qualifications | Only accredited institutions award degrees; a certificate of completion is not accreditation | Don't describe your certificate as a regulated qualification unless it is one | Degrees are awarded by accredited institutions; a short course certificate is not a degree |
| Student data | Privacy policy; state privacy laws where they apply | UK GDPR | Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 ([NDPC](https://ndpc.gov.ng/)) |

:::callout tip "Set up the UK cancellation checkbox"
If you sell to UK buyers and give instant access, add an unticked checkbox at checkout: "I want immediate access and understand I lose my 14-day right to cancel." Include it in the order confirmation. Without it, buyers can still cancel within 14 days.
:::

## Step 5: Budget your costs

Planning estimates for a solo creator, not quotes.

| Cost item | Planning estimate (US / UK) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Microphone | $60–$200 / £50–£180 | Audio quality matters more than video quality |
| Camera or webcam, lighting | $0–$400 / £0–£350 | A recent phone and window light are fine to start |
| Editing software | $0–$30 a month | Free editors work for simple lessons |
| Course platform | Monthly subscription or revenue share | Compare fees at your expected sales volume |
| Website and landing pages | $100–$3,000 / £80–£2,500 | Or a custom site if you plan multiple courses |
| Email marketing tool | Free to start, then by list size | Essential for launches |
| Business registration | Free (US EIN) to £100 (UK company) | Nigerian CAC fees are shown on the CAC portal at payment |

In Nigeria, add mobile data or broadband for uploads, a backup power source for live sessions, and payment gateway fees. Price in naira for local learners and in dollars or pounds for international buyers.

## Step 6: Price around the outcome

**Worked example (replace with your numbers):**

- Course price: $199. Platform and payment fees: about 10%, so you keep about **$179** per sale.
- Your launch email goes to a list of 1,500 people. If 2% buy, that's 30 sales.
- 30 × $179 = **$5,370** from one launch.
- Add a live cohort option at $499 for 10 students: 10 × $449 (after fees) = **$4,490**.
- Total launch revenue: **$9,860**, before ad spend.

The lesson: list size and conversion rate matter more than the number of lessons. Grow the list between launches with a free resource and a simple [sales funnel](/glossary/sales-funnel/).

:::myth-fact
Myth: More hours of video make a course more valuable.
Fact: Learners pay for the result. A shorter course that gets them there faster is often worth more to them.
Myth: If you build it on a big marketplace, students will come.
Fact: Marketplaces have huge competition and control your pricing. Your own audience, email list and website are the channels you control.
:::

## E-learning business FAQs

:::faq
### How much does it cost to start an online course business?
You can start with a phone, a good microphone, free editing software and a course platform, often for a few hundred dollars or pounds. Costs rise if you buy studio gear, a custom website or paid ads.
### Do I need special qualifications to create an online course?
No formal qualification is needed to teach a skill, but you need real expertise and results to show. Don't describe your certificate as accredited or regulated unless it is.
### Which platform is best for hosting my online course?
The one your learners can use easily, with mobile apps, offline downloads, local payment options and the ability to export your student list. Pair it with your own website for search traffic and branding.
### How do I validate a course idea?
Pre-sell a live pilot to a small group with a minimum number of buyers set in advance. If people pay, build the full course from the pilot. If they don't, adjust the topic, promise or price.
### Can UK customers get a refund on my online course?
Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, buyers can cancel digital content within 14 days unless they expressly asked for immediate access and acknowledged losing that right. Record that consent at checkout.
:::
