To start a freelancing business, choose a niche you can prove with past work, register for tax, price by project or monthly retainer, use a contract for every job, and build a client pipeline you own alongside marketplaces like Upwork and Fiverr. The difference between freelancing as a gig and freelancing as a business is structure: a clear offer, written terms, separate finances and a steady source of leads.

This guide shows how to start a freelancing business in the US, the UK and Nigeria, with tax rules, pricing maths, ways to get paid across borders and how to find clients.

:::takeaways
- Freelancing is self-employment. Register for tax from your first meaningful income.
- Price from a target yearly income and your realistic billable hours, not from marketplace averages.
- A contract and a deposit protect you from the two classic problems: scope creep and late payment.
- Marketplaces are a good start, but a website, referrals and an email list give you clients nobody can switch off.
:::

## How do you legally start a freelance business?

**United States.** You are a sole proprietor by default as soon as you freelance. You can stay that way or form an LLC with your state ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/register-your-business)). Get a [free EIN from the IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number) to use on client tax forms instead of your Social Security number. Self-employment tax of 15.3% (Social Security and Medicare) applies once net earnings reach $400 ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employment-tax-social-security-and-medicare-taxes)), so set money aside from every invoice.

**United Kingdom.** Register as a sole trader with HMRC if you earn more than £1,000 from self-employment in a tax year; the tax year runs from 6 April to 5 April ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-sole-trader)). Income tax applies above the £12,570 Personal Allowance ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/income-tax-rates)), and Class 4 National Insurance is 6% on profits between £12,570 and £50,270 ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/self-employed-national-insurance-rates)). VAT registration is required if taxable turnover passes £90,000 in 12 months ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration/when-to-register)).

**Nigeria.** Register a business name with the [CAC](https://www.cac.gov.ng/) so you can open a business account and invoice companies professionally. Under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, personal income tax starts at 0% on the first ₦800,000 and 15% on the next ₦2,200,000 ([Aluko & Oyebode, 2025](https://aluko-oyebode.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Overview-of-the-Notable-Changes-Introduced-by-the-New-Nigeria-Tax-Acts-2025.pdf)). If you earn in dollars or pounds, use a payout route that lets you show where the money came from; it simplifies tax and bank compliance.

## How should you price freelance work?

Start with the income you need, then divide by the hours you can realistically bill. Worked example (illustrative), UK designer:

- Target income before tax: £40,000.
- Business costs (software, insurance, equipment): £4,000.
- Revenue needed: £40,000 + £4,000 = **£44,000**.
- Billable hours: 46 working weeks × 25 billable hours = **1,150 hours** (the rest goes on sales, admin and learning).
- Minimum rate: £44,000 ÷ 1,150 = **£38.26 an hour**, so quote at least £40.

Then convert to project prices. A logo and brand kit you deliver in 20 hours is at least 20 × £40 = £800. Quoting the project, not the hours, lets you keep the upside when you get faster.

The same method works in naira or dollars. Nigerian freelancers serving foreign clients should price in the client's currency and know their effective naira rate after platform and transfer fees.

## How to start a freelancing business: step by step

:::steps "From first gig to steady business"
1. Define your offer | One service, one client type, one clear outcome. Write it as a headline.
2. Build a portfolio of three | Three relevant samples beat thirty unrelated ones. Do spec or discounted work if you must.
3. Register and separate finances | Tax registration, a business bank account and an invoicing tool.
4. Write a contract template | Scope, revisions, timeline, payment terms (deposit plus milestones), ownership transfer on full payment.
5. Set up two lead sources | One marketplace profile and one channel you own (website, LinkedIn, referrals).
6. Deliver, then ask | Ask every satisfied client for a testimonial and a referral within a week of delivery.
7. Add retainers | Offer ongoing support after projects. Retainers turn freelancing into a business.
:::

## How do freelancers find steady clients?

- **Marketplaces (Upwork, Fiverr, Contra).** Good for early reviews. Write a specific profile headline and send fewer, tailored proposals.
- **LinkedIn.** Post work samples and short lessons for your buyer, not for other freelancers.
- **Your own website.** A simple site with services, case studies and a contact form makes referrals convert. [TechDella Studios](studios:/services/website-design/) can build it, and our [branding team](studios:/services/branding/) can sharpen how you look.
- **Referral partners.** Agencies and complementary freelancers overflow work. A designer needs a copywriter; a developer needs a designer.
- **A simple lead magnet.** A checklist or template your buyers want, in exchange for an email. Our [lead generation](/services/lead-generation/) page shows how businesses structure this.

:::faq
### How do I start freelancing with no experience?
Pick a skill you can already do to a professional standard, create three portfolio samples (personal projects or discounted work), and start with small fixed-price jobs to earn reviews. Raise prices after each set of good reviews.
### Do freelancers need to register a business?
In the US you are a sole proprietor automatically, but you still report income and pay self-employment tax. In the UK you must register for Self Assessment above £1,000 a year. In Nigeria, a CAC business name makes invoicing and banking easier.
### How much should I charge as a freelancer?
Divide your target income plus business costs by your realistic billable hours (often 1,000 to 1,200 a year). Use that as your floor, then quote by project.
### Is Fiverr or Upwork better for beginners?
Fiverr suits packaged services with a fixed price. Upwork suits proposals for custom projects. Many freelancers use one to earn early reviews, then move clients to direct contracts where the platform's terms allow.
### How do Nigerian freelancers get paid by foreign clients?
Through marketplace payouts, international payment providers or domiciliary accounts. Compare fees and exchange rates, keep invoices and records for every payment, and price in the client's currency.
:::
