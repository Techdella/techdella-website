To start a cyber cafe business in 2026, don't build it around browsing time. Most people now go online on their phones. What they still can't do easily on a phone is print, scan, fill long online forms, upload documents in the right format, design a CV, or work for a few hours with fast internet and steady power. That's where a modern cyber cafe makes its money.

So the plan is: choose a location with walk-in demand, set up reliable power and internet, and sell a menu of digital services with clear prices. This guide covers the setup, costs, revenue maths and marketing.

:::takeaways
- Browsing is a small slice of income now. Printing, registrations and document services carry the business.
- Location decides everything: near schools, exam centers, offices, markets or government offices.
- Power and internet are your product. Budget for backup power and a second connection.
- A clear price list and fast service beat cheap prices.
:::

## Is a cyber cafe still a good business?

It depends on where you are. In the US and UK, almost everyone is online at home, so standalone cyber cafes are rare outside gaming lounges and coworking spaces. In Nigeria, internet use is growing fast, but a large share of the population is still not online, and many people who are online lack printers, laptops or stable power.

## What services make money in a cyber cafe?

Think of your price list as several small [revenue streams](/glossary/revenue-stream/). Most successful cafes and business centers offer:

- **Printing, photocopying and scanning:** steady, high-volume, decent margin.
- **Online registrations and applications:** exam registrations, school portals, job applications, visa forms and government portals. Charge a service fee for the time and know-how.
- **Document services:** CV writing and formatting, typing, PDF conversion, lamination and binding.
- **Passport photos:** a camera, a backdrop and the right print size.
- **Work desks:** fast internet and steady power by the hour or day for remote workers and students.
- **Browsing and gaming:** still useful, but plan for it to be a smaller share.
- **Basic training:** short computer and internet skills classes on quiet days.

## How to start a cyber cafe business, step by step

:::steps
1. Pick the location | Count foot traffic near schools, exam centers, offices or government buildings. Visibility and safety matter more than low rent.
2. Decide your service mix | Use the list above. Ask nearby businesses and students what they can't get done easily.
3. Register the business | CAC business name or company in Nigeria; local business registration elsewhere. Ask your local government about trading permits and signage.
4. Sort power first | Plan backup power: a solar or inverter system, a generator, or both. Downtime costs customers.
5. Get two internet connections | One main link and one backup from a different provider, so an outage doesn't close you.
6. Buy equipment | Computers, a heavy-duty printer, scanner, photocopier, router, UPS units, furniture and a camera for photos.
7. Set up security and software | User accounts, auto-wiping browser sessions, antivirus, and a timer or billing system.
8. Publish a price list | Clear prices on the wall and on your WhatsApp catalog and Google profile.
9. Open and promote | Launch offers for students and nearby offices, and ask early customers for reviews.
:::

## Revenue maths: a sample day

Illustrative numbers for a 6-computer center. Use your local prices.

- Printing and copying: 400 pages × 50 = **20,000**
- Online registrations and forms: 12 × 1,500 = **18,000**
- CVs and typing: 4 × 2,500 = **10,000**
- Work desk and browsing: 20 hours × 400 = **8,000**
- Passport photos: 6 × 1,000 = **6,000**
- **Daily revenue: 62,000**

Costs per day: paper and toner 6,000, fuel or power 9,000, internet 3,000, rent and staff 20,000 = **38,000**. Profit before tax: 62,000 − 38,000 = **24,000 a day**. Over 26 trading days that's 24,000 × 26 = **624,000 a month**.

Notice what drives the profit: registrations and document services, not browsing. That's the [business model](/glossary/business-model/) to build around.

## How to market a cyber cafe

- **Be visible on maps.** A Google Business Profile with your hours, services and photos is how people searching "printing near me" or "cyber cafe near me" find you. See how [local SEO](/glossary/local-seo/) works, or let our [local SEO](/services/local-seo/) team help.
- **Use WhatsApp Business.** Post your price list as a catalog and let people send documents before they arrive.
- **Partner with schools and offices.** Offer monthly printing accounts to nearby businesses and bulk rates to student groups.
- **Watch the calendar.** Exam registrations, school resumption and job recruitment drives bring rushes. Extend hours and add staff.

## Common mistakes new cyber cafe owners make

- **Buying too many computers on day one.** Start with the number you can keep busy at peak times, and add machines when customers wait.
- **A cheap printer.** Light-duty printers break under daily volume. Buy for pages per month and cost per page, not sticker price.
- **No staff training on portals.** Customers come for help with forms. Train every attendant on the common registration and application portals, and keep a written checklist for each.
- **Unclear prices.** Haggling at the counter slows service and annoys regulars. Post prices and stick to them.
- **Ignoring security.** Shared machines attract misuse. Lock admin access, update antivirus and keep a visitor log during busy periods.

:::faq
### Is a cyber cafe business still profitable in 2026?
It can be, in the right location and with the right services. Cafes that rely on browsing alone struggle. Those built around printing, online registrations, document services and work desks near schools and offices can earn steadily.
### How much do I need to start a cyber cafe?
A small center with a few computers, a heavy-duty printer, backup power and two internet links can start modestly, especially with refurbished computers. Power and printing equipment are the costs most people underestimate.
### What services make the most money in a cyber cafe?
Usually online registrations and applications, printing and copying, and document services such as CVs. They need know-how or equipment customers don't have at home, so people pay a fair service fee.
### Do I need a license to run a cyber cafe in Nigeria?
Register the business with CAC and check with your local government about trading permits and signage. Requirements can differ by state and local government area, so ask before you sign a lease.
### How do I keep customers' data safe?
Set up separate user accounts, clear browser data and downloads after each session, never save customer passwords, and delete scanned documents once you've finished. Customers notice and come back to places they trust.
:::
