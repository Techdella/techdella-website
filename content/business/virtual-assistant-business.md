To start a virtual assistant business, choose a specialty, register for tax, sell monthly retainers in blocks of hours, put data protection and account access rules in writing, and win clients through referrals, LinkedIn and partner businesses. General admin is the most crowded corner of the market and the most exposed to automation. Specialist VAs who run a process end to end (a founder's inbox, an online store's operations, a law firm's intake) are the ones clients keep.

This guide covers how to start a virtual assistant business in the US, the UK and Nigeria, including pricing, contracts, tools and getting clients across borders.

:::takeaways
- Specialize. Pay for administrative work varies a lot by specialty, and so do VA rates.
- Sell monthly hour blocks or outcome-based packages, not ad hoc hours.
- Clients trust you with passwords and personal data. Use a password manager and a written data agreement.
- Learn automation tools. Clients pay more for a VA who removes work, not just does it.
:::

## Which virtual assistant services should you offer?

| Specialty | What you run | Typical client | Tools to know |

| Ecommerce operations | Orders, customer service, listings, returns | Online stores | Shopify, marketplace dashboards, help desks |
| Marketing support | Scheduling posts, newsletters, reporting | Coaches, small businesses | Social schedulers, email platforms |

Choose one specialty to lead your marketing. You can still help with other tasks, but your website and profile should sell one clear service.

## How do you register a VA business?

**United States.** You can operate as a sole proprietor or form an LLC ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/register-your-business)). Use a [free EIN](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number) on client paperwork. Set aside money for self-employment tax, which is 15.3% once net earnings reach $400 ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employment-tax-social-security-and-medicare-taxes)).

**United Kingdom.** Register as a sole trader once you earn more than £1,000 in a tax year from self-employment ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-sole-trader)). Because you handle clients' personal data, check whether you need to pay the [ICO data protection fee](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/). Keep VAT in mind: you must register once taxable turnover passes £90,000 in 12 months ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration/when-to-register)).

**Nigeria.** Register a business name with the [CAC](https://www.cac.gov.ng/) and open a business account. Many Nigerian VAs serve clients in the US and UK. If you handle their customers' data, expect to sign data processing terms, and follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 for any Nigerian data ([NDPC](https://ndpc.gov.ng/)). Plan reliable power and internet backup; it is part of your service level.

## How should you price virtual assistant services?

Sell monthly packages, priced from a target hourly rate, with a small premium for priority response. Worked example (illustrative):

- Package: 20 hours a month of executive support.
- Target rate: $35 an hour → 20 × $35 = **$700**.
- Priority response (same business day): add 10% → $700 × 1.10 = **$770** a month.

Five clients on that package bring in 5 × $770 × 12 = **$46,200** a year for about 100 client hours a month, leaving time for admin and sales.

A Nigerian VA serving US clients might price lower than a US-based VA and still earn well above local salaries. Price in the client's currency, and compare that to what a local hire would cost the client, not to what other VAs on marketplaces charge.

## How to start a virtual assistant business: step by step

:::steps "From first client to full roster"
1. Pick your specialty | One service line you can explain in a sentence.
2. Document your processes | Write how you run an inbox, a calendar or an order queue. Clients buy confidence.
3. Register and set up payments | Tax registration, business account, invoicing, international payment route.
4. Write a VA agreement | Hours, response times, access rules, confidentiality, data handling, notice period.
5. Set up secure access | Password manager sharing, separate logins per client, two-factor authentication.
6. Land two pilot clients | Discount the first month in exchange for testimonials you can quote word for word.
7. Add automation | Use automation tools to remove repetitive tasks, then sell that as a higher-value package.
:::

## How do virtual assistants find clients?

- **Referrals.** Former managers and colleagues know your work. Tell them exactly what you now offer.
- **LinkedIn.** Write for the people who hire you (founders, executives, store owners), not for other VAs.
- **Partner businesses.** Bookkeepers, agencies and coaches often need overflow admin. A partner can send several clients.
- **A simple website.** One page per specialty, a process section, testimonials and a booking link. [TechDella Studios](studios:/services/website-design/) can build it.
- **Automation as a door-opener.** Many small businesses want [marketing automation](/glossary/marketing-automation/) but lack the time. VAs who can set up and run it stand out; see how we approach [marketing automation](/services/marketing-automation/).

:::myth-fact
Myth: AI will replace virtual assistants.
Fact: AI replaces routine tasks. VAs who own a process, use AI tools well and handle judgment calls become more valuable to small businesses, not less.
Myth: Clients care most about your hourly rate.
Fact: Clients care most about reliability, response times and trust with their data. A clear process and security setup justify a higher rate.

:::faq
### How do I start a virtual assistant business with no experience?
Use the admin, customer service or organizing skills you already have from work or volunteering. Offer a pilot month at a discount to build testimonials, and document your processes so clients can see how you work.
### How much should a virtual assistant charge?
Set a target hourly rate from your income goal and realistic hours, then sell monthly packages. Specialist VAs (executive, legal, ecommerce) can charge more than general admins.
### Do I need to register my VA business?
Yes, for tax. US VAs report self-employment income; UK VAs register for Self Assessment above £1,000 a year; Nigerian VAs benefit from a CAC business name for banking and invoicing.
### Can I be a virtual assistant from Nigeria for US or UK clients?
Yes. Many do. Plan for time zones, reliable power and internet, international payments and the data protection terms your clients will ask you to sign.
### What tools do virtual assistants need?
A reliable laptop, a password manager, video calls, a shared task tracker, time tracking and invoicing software. Add the tools your specialty uses, such as Shopify or a CRM.
:::
