To start a wedding planning business, get real experience first, ideally by assisting an established planner, then choose your market (budget, style and location), package your services, register and insure the business, and build relationships with venues and vendors who will refer couples to you. A wedding planning business runs on trust, so your contracts, communication and reviews matter as much as your eye for design.

This guide covers how to start a wedding planning business in the US, the UK and Nigeria, with packages, pricing maths and the paperwork that protects you.

:::takeaways
- Weddings are big-budget, high-emotion projects. Couples hire planners to reduce stress and avoid costly mistakes.
- Offer three clear packages: full planning, partial planning and day-of coordination.
- Charge a flat or percentage fee and disclose any vendor commissions.
- Venues and vendors are your best referral source. Treat them as partners.
:::

Location changes everything. Budgets in one city can be more than double those in another, so research your local market before setting prices. In Nigeria, large guest lists, traditional and white weddings on separate days, and diaspora couples planning a wedding at home from abroad all create demand for skilled coordination. ## Step 1: Get experience before you launch

Weddings have no second takes. Before you sell your own services:

- **Assist an established planner** for at least a season. You'll see contracts, vendor management and crisis handling up close.
- **Coordinate for friends and family**, and document it with photos and a timeline.
- **Take a course** in event planning or wedding planning if you want structure; it's not legally required in any of the three countries.

Our wider guide to [starting an event planning business](/business/event-planning-business/) covers the general event skills.

## Step 2: Choose your packages

| Package | What's included | Typical timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Full planning | Budget, venue search, vendor selection and contracts, design, guest management, timeline, on-the-day management | 9–18 months |
| Partial planning | Couple books some vendors; you handle the rest, design and the day | 4–6 months |
| Day-of coordination | Final vendor confirmations, timeline, rehearsal and on-the-day management | 6–8 weeks before |
| Add-ons | Destination logistics, traditional ceremony coordination, guest travel, welcome events | As needed |

Write down what each package excludes. Extra site visits, additional events and out-of-area travel should be priced separately.

## Step 3: Register, insure and protect yourself

| | United States | United Kingdom | Nigeria |
|---|---|---|---|
| Licence | No specific state licence for wedding planners; check local business licence rules ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/apply-licenses-permits)) | No licence needed to plan weddings; legal ceremonies must follow marriage law and approved-venue rules | No licence for planners; register the business and keep contracts formal |
| Business registration | LLC or sole proprietorship; free EIN ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number)) | Sole trader (Self Assessment once trading income tops £1,000) or £100 limited company ([Companies House](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company)) | Business name or company with the [CAC](https://www.cac.gov.ng/); TIN |
| Insurance | General liability and professional liability (errors and omissions) | Public liability and professional indemnity | Ask about professional and event liability cover |
| Tax | Sales tax rules vary by state for services | VAT once turnover passes £90,000 ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration/when-to-register)) | Keep records of all deposits and vendor payments |

:::callout warning "Contracts protect friendships too"
Use a written contract for every couple, including friends. Cover scope, payment schedule, cancellation and postponement terms, vendor commissions, your hours on the day, and what happens if you're ill. Have a lawyer review the template once.
:::

## Step 4: Budget your startup costs

Wedding planning is low-capital. These are planning estimates, not quotes.

| Cost item | Planning estimate (US / UK) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Business registration and contract template | $200–$1,500 / £150–£1,200 | A lawyer-reviewed contract is the best money you'll spend |
| Insurance (first year) | $400–$2,000 / £300–£1,500 | Venues often require proof |
| Website, branding and portfolio photos | $500–$5,000 / £400–£4,000 | Real wedding photos sell; get photographer permission |
| Planning software | $0–$50 a month | Timelines, budgets, guest lists, client portal |
| Emergency kit for the day | $100–$300 / £80–£250 | Sewing kit, stain remover, first aid, chargers |
| Styled shoot or showcase | $0–$2,000 / £0–£1,500 | Optional; collaborate with vendors to share costs |
| Bridal shows and directories | $0–$2,000 / £0–£1,500 | Test one before committing to many |

In Nigeria, budget for transport between vendors and venues, a team of assistants for large guest lists, a power plan for venues with unreliable supply, and payment arrangements that suit diaspora couples paying in foreign currency.

## Step 5: Price your services

Planners usually charge a flat fee, a percentage of the wedding budget, or a mix.

**Worked example (replace with your numbers):**

- A full-planning wedding takes you about 150 hours over 12 months.
- Your target rate is $40 an hour: 150 × $40 = **$6,000** flat fee.
- As a check, at a $34,200 budget, a 15% fee would be about $5,130; at 18%, about $6,160.
- Day-of coordination takes about 35 hours: 35 × $40 = **$1,400**.

If you plan 8 full weddings and 12 day-of coordinations a year, that's 8 × $6,000 + 12 × $1,400 = **$64,800** in fees, from about 1,620 hours of work. Weekends cluster in peak season, so check you can physically be at every wedding you book, or hire a trained lead coordinator.

:::myth-fact
Myth: Wedding planners get paid mainly through vendor commissions.
Fact: A clear planning fee paid by the couple is the core of a sustainable business. If you also accept vendor commissions, disclose them; hidden commissions damage trust and can breach your contract.
Myth: You need a certification to be a wedding planner.
Fact: No licence or certification is legally required in the US, UK or Nigeria. Experience, a portfolio and referrals matter far more to couples.
:::

## Step 6: Win your first couples

:::steps "First six months"
1. Build a portfolio | Photos and timelines from weddings you assisted or coordinated, with permission.
2. Meet 10 venues | Introduce yourself to venue coordinators and leave a one-page service sheet.
3. Build a vendor circle | Photographers, florists, caterers, MCs and decorators who refer each other.
4. Launch your website | Packages, starting prices, a gallery and an enquiry form.
5. Collect reviews | Ask every couple for a review on Google and wedding directories.
6. Follow up every enquiry within a day | Speed and warmth win bookings.
:::

## Wedding planning business FAQs

:::faq
### Do I need certification to start a wedding planning business?
No. There's no legal certification requirement in the US, UK or Nigeria. A course can help structure your knowledge, but couples hire planners based on experience, portfolio, reviews and referrals.
### How much do beginner wedding planners earn?
It depends on your market, packages and how many weddings you can handle. Work out your hourly target, estimate the hours for each package, and price from there. Day-of coordination is often the first paid service new planners sell.
### Can I start a wedding planning business from home?
Yes. Most planners work from home and meet couples at venues, cafés or online. You'll need a professional website, reliable communication tools and transport for site visits.
### How do I get my first wedding planning clients?
Start with your network and weddings you've assisted on, build relationships with venues and vendors who can refer couples, and make sure couples can find your reviews and gallery online.
### Should I charge a flat fee or a percentage?
Flat fees are easier for couples to understand and protect you from low budgets. Percentage fees scale with the wedding's complexity. A flat fee with a stated minimum is a simple middle ground.
:::
