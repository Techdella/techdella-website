To start a fitness business, choose a model (independent personal training, small-group classes, online coaching or your own studio), hold a recognized certification plus CPR and first aid, insure the business, price packages or memberships from your available hours, and build a base of recurring clients before you sign a lease. Premises are the most expensive mistake new fitness owners make. Prove demand with a client list first.

This guide covers how to start a fitness business in the US, the UK and Nigeria, with startup costs, pricing maths and how to keep clients beyond January.

:::takeaways
- Start lean: train clients in their homes, parks, a rented studio slot or online before signing a lease.
- Certification is expected by clients and insurers even where the law doesn't require it.
- Memberships and packages beat pay-per-session. Recurring revenue is what makes a fitness business stable.
- Small-group training raises your income per hour without raising your hours.
:::

## Which fitness business model should you choose?

| Model | Startup cost | Income per hour | Capacity limit | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Independent personal training (client homes, parks, rented space) | Low | High per client | Your hours | Experienced trainers with a network |
| Small-group training (4–10 people) | Low to medium | Higher | Space and coaching quality | Trainers who like community |
| Online coaching (programs, check-ins, app) | Low | Medium to high | Your support time | Trainers with content skills |
| Corporate wellness | Low | High | Contracts | Trainers with B2B confidence |
| Boutique studio or gym | High | Varies | Floor space, members | Owners with proven demand and capital |

Online coaching works in all three countries, but it suits the UK and US more for paid acquisition. In Nigeria, community classes, estate and office partnerships, and WhatsApp-based programs are common routes.

## What certifications and registration do you need?

**Certification.** Most trainers hold certification in their specialty, personal trainers usually must be certified before working with gym clients, and most also need CPR, first aid and AED certification ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/personal-care-and-service/fitness-trainers-and-instructors.htm)). Insurers and corporate clients usually ask to see these.

**United States.** Register the business with your state and get a [free EIN](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number). Parks and public spaces may require permits for commercial classes; check local licensing via the [SBA](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/apply-licenses-permits).

**United Kingdom.** Register as a sole trader once self-employment income tops £1,000 in a tax year ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-sole-trader)). Councils often require permission for paid group training in parks. If you employ coaches, you need employers' liability insurance of at least £5 million ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/employers-liability-insurance)).

**Nigeria.** Register with the [CAC](https://www.cac.gov.ng/). If you train in estates or offices, agree access, liability and payment terms in writing with the estate or company.

**Health screening.** Use a pre-exercise health questionnaire with every new client, and refer clients to a doctor when it flags a risk. Stay within your scope: don't prescribe diets or treat injuries unless qualified.

## How should you price fitness services?

Move clients onto monthly packages as early as possible. Worked example (illustrative), UK trainer comparing one-to-one with small groups:

- One-to-one: £45 a session. Twenty sessions a week = 20 × £45 = **£900** a week.
- Small group: 6 people × £15 each = £90 a session. Ten group sessions a week = 10 × £90 = **£900** a week.
- Same revenue, but the small-group schedule uses half the hours, leaving time for online clients or rest.

Monthly membership view: 40 small-group members paying £79 a month for unlimited classes is 40 × £79 = **£3,160** a month, or **£37,920** a year, before costs and tax. A [subscription model](/glossary/subscription-model/) smooths the January spike and the summer dip.

Online coaching adds income without adding sessions: 15 clients at £99 a month is **£1,485** a month for programming and weekly check-ins.

UK sole traders pay Class 4 National Insurance at 6% on profits between £12,570 and £50,270 ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/self-employed-national-insurance-rates)); US trainers pay 15.3% self-employment tax ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employment-tax-social-security-and-medicare-taxes)); Nigerian personal income tax starts at 0% on the first ₦800,000 ([Aluko & Oyebode, 2025](https://aluko-oyebode.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Overview-of-the-Notable-Changes-Introduced-by-the-New-Nigeria-Tax-Acts-2025.pdf)).

## How to start a fitness business: step by step

:::steps "Launch without a lease"
1. Pick a model and a client | For example, "strength training for women over 40" or "corporate lunchtime classes".
2. Get certified and insured | Specialty certification, CPR/first aid/AED, liability insurance.
3. Register the business | And separate business and personal money.
4. Find low-cost space | Client homes, parks with permission, rented studio hours, office meeting rooms.
5. Build packages | A starter offer, a monthly package and an online option.
6. Sign 15 founding clients | Offer a founding rate locked for a year in exchange for commitment and testimonials.
7. Track retention | Measure attendance and cancellations monthly. Act on anyone missing two weeks.
:::

:::faq
### How much does it cost to start a fitness business?
An independent trainer can start with a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars or pounds for certification, insurance and portable equipment. A studio costs far more because of rent, fit-out and equipment.
### Do I need a certification to be a personal trainer?
In practice, yes. Most clients, gyms and insurers expect a recognized certification plus CPR and first aid. Requirements are set by employers and insurers more than by law.
### How much should a personal trainer charge?
Base your rate on local market prices, your hours and your costs, then sell monthly packages rather than single sessions. Small-group sessions let you charge less per person and earn more per hour.
### Is an online fitness business profitable?
It can be, because delivery costs are low. The challenge is acquiring clients and keeping them engaged. Programs with regular check-ins retain better than downloadable plans.
### When should I open a studio?
When your existing clients and waitlist would cover rent and running costs, ideally with a few months of reserves. Treat a waitlist as your signal, not a hunch.
:::
