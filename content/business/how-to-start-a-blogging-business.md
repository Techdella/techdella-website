To start a blogging business, choose a topic where you have first-hand experience, publish articles that fully answer what people search for, build an email list from the first week, and earn from several streams: display ads, affiliate links, sponsorships and your own products or services. A blog becomes a business when income no longer depends on one traffic source or one revenue stream.

This guide covers how to start a blogging business in 2026, when Google rewards experience and helpfulness, AI answers summarize the web, and readers in Nigeria, the US and the UK have more content than ever to choose from.

:::takeaways
- Experience is the moat. Write about what you have done, tested or measured, not what you have read.
- Search traffic is volatile. An email list is the audience you own.
- Ads alone rarely make a blog a business. Combine affiliates, sponsorships and your own offers.
- Disclose affiliate and sponsored links clearly; regulators in the US and UK expect it.
:::

## How do you choose a profitable blog topic?

Pick a topic that sits where three things overlap: your experience, an audience with problems to solve, and products or services that audience buys.

| Topic test | Good sign | Warning sign |
|---|---|---|
| Your experience | You have done it, for years or at depth | You would be summarizing other blogs |
| Audience problems | People ask specific "how do I" questions | Questions are answered fully in one AI reply |
| Buying intent | Readers buy tools, courses, services or gear | Readers rarely spend money on the topic |
| Competition | Big sites cover it broadly, not deeply | Dominated by brands with first-hand data |
| Longevity | Problems recur every year | One-time trend |

Examples that tend to work: personal finance for Nigerian professionals abroad, home renovation on a UK budget, software tutorials for a specific job, parenting a child with a specific condition. Broad "lifestyle" blogs are hard to monetize.

## How do you write a blog that ranks and gets cited by AI?

Google's guidance asks creators to make helpful, reliable, people-first content and to show who wrote it and why they're qualified ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)). Mass-produced pages made mainly to rank break Google's spam policies on scaled content abuse ([Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)).

In practice:

- **Answer first.** Put the direct answer in the first two or three sentences. It helps readers, featured snippets and AI answers.
- **Show your work.** Screenshots, your own numbers, photos, mistakes and what you would do differently.
- **Write clearly.** Short sentences and plain words. Check drafts with our free [readability checker](/tools/free-readability-checker/).
- **Structure for scanning.** Descriptive headings, lists, tables and a summary.
- **Link internally.** Connect related posts so readers and crawlers find your best work.

## How do bloggers make money?

| Revenue stream | When it starts working | Effort | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display ads (AdSense or ad networks) | Once you have steady traffic | Low ongoing | Publisher keeps 80% of AdSense content revenue after the advertiser platform fee |
| Affiliate links | Early, on product-focused posts | Medium | Must be disclosed clearly |
| Sponsored posts and newsletters | After you have an engaged audience | Medium | Label as advertising |
| Digital products (templates, guides, courses) | After you know readers' problems | High upfront | Highest margins |
| Services (consulting, coaching, freelance) | Early | High | Blog acts as your portfolio and lead source |

[Affiliate marketing](/glossary/affiliate-marketing/) is usually the first meaningful stream. Disclose it. The FTC says bloggers should disclose relationships with retailers clearly and conspicuously, close to the recommendation ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking)). In the UK, the CAP Code requires marketing to be obviously identifiable, and the ASA has found that a vague "affiliate" label or a disclaimer at the bottom of a post is unlikely to be enough ([ASA](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/affiliate-marketing.html)).

## A worked example: what does a blog need to earn $2,000 a month?

Illustrative numbers, not a forecast. Suppose a personal finance blog has these streams:

- Display ads: 40,000 monthly pageviews at an assumed $15 earned per 1,000 pageviews → 40 × $15 = **$600**.
- Affiliate: 400 clicks to partners, 5% convert, $40 commission → 400 × 0.05 × $40 = **$800**.
- One sponsored newsletter a month: **$300**.
- A $25 budgeting template, 12 sales → 12 × $25 = **$300**.
- Total: $600 + $800 + $300 + $300 = **$2,000** a month.

Ad rates vary widely by topic, season and reader country; ad rates for readers in Nigeria are often lower than for readers in the US or UK, which is why Nigerian bloggers often lean on affiliates, sponsorships and their own products. Plug in your own numbers once you have a few months of data.

## How to start a blogging business: step by step

:::steps "Your first six months"
1. Choose your topic and reader | One reader, one set of problems you can solve from experience.
2. Research 50 questions | List the questions your reader searches and asks in forums and comments.
3. Set up the site | Fast hosting, a clean theme, an about page that shows your experience.
4. Publish 20 strong posts | Answer-first, with your own examples. Quality over volume.
5. Start the email list | A simple sign-up offer: a checklist, template or short course.
6. Add the first revenue stream | Usually affiliates on your most product-focused posts, clearly disclosed.
7. Register for tax | Once income starts: US self-employment tax from $400 of net earnings ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employment-tax-social-security-and-medicare-taxes)), UK Self Assessment above £1,000 ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-sole-trader)), a CAC business name in Nigeria ([CAC](https://www.cac.gov.ng/)).
:::

- **Email.** Send one useful email a week. Our [email marketing service](/services/email-marketing/) shows how welcome and nurture sequences work.
- **Repurpose.** Turn each post into a short video, a carousel and a thread.
- **Collaborate.** Guest appearances on podcasts and newsletters in your niche.
- **Community.** A WhatsApp, Telegram or Discord group for your most engaged readers.

:::myth-fact
Myth: Publishing lots of AI-written posts is the fastest way to grow.
Fact: Google's spam policies target scaled content made mainly to manipulate rankings. Fewer posts with real experience and original detail are safer and more useful.
Myth: A small disclaimer at the bottom of the post covers affiliate links.
Fact: UK regulators say end-of-post or vague "may earn commission" disclaimers are unlikely to be enough, and the FTC wants disclosures close to the recommendation.

:::faq
### How much does it cost to start a blog?
A basic blog can start for under $100 or £80 a year for a domain and hosting. Design, email tools and SEO tools add cost as you grow.
### How long does it take to make money blogging?
Most blogs take months before search traffic is steady. Affiliate income can start earlier on product-focused posts. Services or consulting can earn from the first month if your blog shows expertise.
### How much does AdSense pay bloggers?
Google pays publishers 80% of AdSense for content revenue after the advertiser platform takes its fee. What you earn per 1,000 pageviews depends on your topic, audience country and season.
### Do I need to register my blog as a business?
Once it earns income, yes for tax. US bloggers pay self-employment tax from $400 of net earnings; UK bloggers register for Self Assessment above £1,000 a year; Nigerian bloggers benefit from a CAC business name for banking and sponsorship invoices.
### Is blogging still worth it with AI search?
Yes for blogs with first-hand experience, original data and an engaged audience. Generic how-to content is harder to sustain because AI answers cover it directly.
:::
