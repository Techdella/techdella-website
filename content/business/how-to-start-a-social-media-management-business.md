To start a social media management business, pick a niche you understand, choose the two platforms your clients' buyers actually use, and sell a fixed monthly package with clear deliverables. Register the business, put a contract in place that covers approvals and account access, and price from the hours each package really takes.

This guide shows how to start a social media management business in Nigeria, the US or the UK, with the numbers, rules and systems that keep a small agency profitable.

:::takeaways
- Sell outcomes on two platforms, not "posting on everything".
- Price monthly retainers from hours per client, and cap revisions and meetings.
- When you post paid or gifted content for clients, ad disclosure rules apply in all three countries.
- Keep client account access in their name. Never own their pages.
:::

## Step 1: Choose a niche and two platforms

The fastest way to stand out is to serve one type of client well. Examples:

- Restaurants and cafés in one city (Instagram and TikTok).
- B2B SaaS founders (LinkedIn and X).
- Real estate developers and agents (Instagram, YouTube and WhatsApp).
- Beauty and wellness brands (Instagram and TikTok).

A niche lets you reuse content frameworks, templates and reporting, which is where your margin comes from. It also makes your case studies more persuasive to the next client in the same niche.

## Step 2: Package your services

Clients buy clear packages faster than custom quotes. Three tiers usually cover it:

| Package | What's included | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 12 posts a month on one platform, captions, scheduling, monthly report | Small local businesses |
| Growth | 16–20 posts on two platforms, 4 short videos, community replies on weekdays, monthly call | Growing brands with some budget |
| Growth + Ads | Growth package plus [paid social](/services/paid-ads/) campaign setup and management (ad spend billed separately) | Brands that want leads, not just reach |

Write down exactly what's not included: photo shoots, influencer fees, crisis management outside working hours, and unlimited revisions.

Ask clients to add you as a manager through each platform's business tools (Meta Business Suite, LinkedIn page admin roles, TikTok Business Center). If the relationship ends, they keep their pages and you avoid disputes.

## Step 4: Price retainers with real maths

Most new managers underprice because they count posting time and forget everything else.

**Worked example for a Growth package (replace with your numbers):**

- Content planning: 3 hours a month.
- Design and video editing: 10 hours.
- Captions and scheduling: 4 hours.
- Community management: 5 hours.
- Reporting and a monthly call: 3 hours.
- Total: **25 hours a month** per client.

If you need to earn $40 an hour after tools and admin time, the package price is 25 × $40 = **$1,000 a month**. Five clients bring in $5,000 a month and need 125 delivery hours, which is about all one person can manage alongside sales and admin. To grow beyond that, you'll need a designer or editor, so build their cost into prices now.

In Nigeria, many small businesses budget in naira and compare against freelancers on Instagram. Lead with a smaller starter package and show results, then upsell. With UK and US clients, price in their currency and invoice through a platform that handles cross-border payments cleanly.

:::myth-fact
Myth: You need to be on every platform to win clients.
Fact: Clients judge you on results. Doing two platforms well is easier to deliver, measure and defend at renewal than doing five badly.
Myth: Follower growth is the main metric clients care about.
Fact: Clients renew when they see enquiries, bookings or sales. Report on those wherever you can track them.
:::

## Step 5: Build your toolkit

Keep it lean at first:

- **Scheduling and inbox:** one tool that covers your two platforms.
- **Design and video:** a design tool with brand kits, plus a mobile video editor.
- **Approvals:** a shared content calendar where clients approve posts before they go out.
- **Reporting:** native platform analytics, plus UTM-tagged links so website visits show in the client's analytics. Our free [hashtag generator](/tools/free-hashtag-generator/) can help with research.
- **Automation:** once you have five clients, automate onboarding forms, reminders and report delivery. That's the kind of system our [marketing automation](/services/marketing-automation/) team sets up.

:::steps "From zero to five clients"
1. Build two sample campaigns | Create a month of content for two businesses in your niche, labelled as samples.
2. Publish your offer | A simple website with your packages, prices or "from" prices, and a booking link.
3. Reach out to 20 businesses a week | Send a short audit of their profile with three specific fixes.

6. Ask for a referral at month three | Happy clients know other owners in the same niche.

## Step 6: Get clients and keep them

Your own presence is your portfolio. Post the kind of content you'd make for clients, and show behind-the-scenes process. A website with a clear offer and a booking form turns that attention into calls.

Retention matters more than acquisition. A client who stays 12 months is worth 12 times their monthly fee, minus onboarding effort, which you only pay once. Hold a monthly review, show results tied to [the client's strategy](/glossary/social-media-marketing-strategy/), and propose one new test each month.

## Social media management business FAQs

:::faq
### How much should I charge for social media management?
Price from hours, not guesses. Add up planning, creation, community management and reporting time for each package, then multiply by your target hourly rate. Ad spend should always be billed separately from your management fee.
### Do I need a degree to start a social media management business?
No. Clients care about results and process. A portfolio of sample campaigns, clear packages and a few client results matter more than a qualification, though short courses in paid social and analytics help.
### How many clients can one social media manager handle?
It depends on package size. At around 25 hours a month per client, one person can usually deliver for four to six clients while still doing sales and admin.
### Do I need to register my social media business?
Yes, once you're trading. Register as a sole trader or company in your country, get a tax ID, and keep business income separate. In the UK, register for Self Assessment once your trading income passes £1,000 a year.
### What's the difference between organic and paid social management?
Organic covers content, posting and community. Paid covers ad campaigns, targeting and budgets. Many clients need both. Our guide to [organic social media](/glossary/organic-social-media/) explains where each fits.
:::
