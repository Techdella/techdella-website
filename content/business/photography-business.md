To start a photography business, choose a niche that clients pay for, register and insure the business, price every session from your total time (shooting, travel and editing), use contracts that set usage rights, and build a portfolio site that turns visitors into inquiries. Most professional photographers work for themselves, so your edge is not access to the job; it is how clearly you sell one kind of photography.

This guide covers how to start a photography business in the US, the UK and Nigeria: niches, costs, registration, pricing, rights and getting booked.

:::takeaways
- Two in three US photographers are self-employed. You are competing with other small businesses, so specialize.
- Price from total hours. Editing and admin often take longer than the shoot.
- Your contract should say who owns the copyright and how the client may use the images.
- A portfolio site organized by niche, with clear "from" prices, books more than a social feed alone.
:::

## Which photography niche should you choose?

Pick a [niche](/glossary/niche-market/) by who pays, how often they book and how much editing each job needs.

| Niche | Who pays | Repeat business | Editing load | Gear beyond basics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weddings | Couples | Low, but strong referrals | Heavy | Second body, fast primes, flash |
| Portraits and family | Families, individuals | Yearly | Medium | Portrait lens, backdrop |
| Headshots and corporate | Companies, professionals | High (new hires, events) | Light to medium | Portable lighting |
| Product and ecommerce | Brands, online stores | High | Medium | Lightbox, macro lens, tethering |
| Events | Companies, organizers | Medium | Medium | Flash, fast zoom |
| Real estate and short-lets | Agents, hosts | High | Light to medium | Wide lens, tripod, sometimes a drone |

Corporate headshots and product photography tend to produce steady repeat work. Weddings pay well per booking but need constant new clients.

## How do you register and protect a photography business?

**United States.** Register as a sole proprietor or LLC with your state ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/register-your-business)) and get a [free EIN](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number). Check city licensing. If you fly a drone for paid work, the FAA's commercial drone rules (Part 107) apply; see [FAA commercial operators](https://www.faa.gov/uas/commercial_operators).

**United Kingdom.** Register as a sole trader once self-employment income passes £1,000 in a tax year ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-sole-trader)), or form a limited company for £100 ([Companies House](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company)). Drone flying for work falls under Civil Aviation Authority rules, including Flyer and Operator IDs ([CAA](https://www.caa.co.uk/drones/rules-and-categories-of-drone-flying/introduction-to-drone-flying-and-the-uk-rules/)).

**Nigeria.** Register a business name with the [CAC](https://www.cac.gov.ng/). Venues, estates and corporate clients increasingly ask for a registered business before they pay invoices.

**Rights and releases.** Put usage rights in every contract: who owns the copyright (usually you), what the client may do with the images, and whether you can use them in your portfolio. Use model releases when you photograph people for commercial use.

## How should you price photography sessions?

Count every hour a session takes, not just the shoot. Worked example (illustrative), US corporate headshot session for a team of 10:

- On site: 3 hours. Travel and setup: 1.5 hours.
- Culling and retouching: 10 images × 20 minutes = 200 minutes, about **3.3 hours**.
- Delivery, invoicing, emails: 1 hour.
- Total: 3 + 1.5 + 3.3 + 1 = **8.8 hours**.
- Target rate: $85 an hour → 8.8 × $85 = **$748**. Add equipment wear and insurance allocation of $80 → **$828**. Quote **$850**, or $85 per person.

Yearly view: two team sessions a week for 46 weeks is 92 × $850 = **$78,200** in revenue before costs and tax. Taxes apply from your first profit: 15.3% self-employment tax in the US ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employment-tax-social-security-and-medicare-taxes)), Class 4 National Insurance at 6% in the UK between £12,570 and £50,270 ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/self-employed-national-insurance-rates)), and personal income tax in Nigeria (0% on the first ₦800,000 under the 2025 Act; [Aluko & Oyebode](https://aluko-oyebode.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Overview-of-the-Notable-Changes-Introduced-by-the-New-Nigeria-Tax-Acts-2025.pdf)).

For weddings and events, take a booking deposit and schedule the balance before the date. Charge extra for additional hours, second shooters, albums and rush delivery.

## How to start a photography business: step by step

:::steps "From portfolio to paid bookings"
1. Choose your niche | One primary niche for your website and marketing. Keep others as referrals.
2. Build a focused portfolio | 20 to 30 of your best images in that niche. Do styled or discounted shoots to fill gaps.
3. Register and insure | Business registration, equipment insurance and public liability cover.
4. Write your contract | Deliverables, timelines, deposit, cancellation, copyright and usage rights.
5. Set packages | Three packages with clear inclusions, plus add-ons.
6. Launch your website | Galleries by niche, "from" prices, testimonials and an inquiry form.
7. Build referral partners | Venues, planners, makeup artists, agencies and estate agents.
:::

:::myth-fact
Myth: Better gear will bring better clients.
Fact: Clients book from your portfolio, reviews and how easy you are to hire. Upgrade gear when a specific niche needs it, not before.
Myth: If a client pays for a shoot, they own the photos.
Fact: In the US and UK the photographer usually owns the copyright unless the contract transfers it. Your contract should say clearly what the client can do with the images.

:::faq
### How much does it cost to start a photography business?
If you already own a camera and lens, you can start with a few hundred dollars or pounds for registration, insurance, software and a website. Starting from scratch with professional gear can cost several thousand.
### Do I need a license to be a photographer?
No photography licence is needed in the US, UK or Nigeria, but you must register the business for tax. Drone photography for paid work has its own rules (FAA Part 107 in the US; CAA rules in the UK).
### How much should a beginner photographer charge?
Work out your total hours per session including editing, multiply by a target hourly rate, and add equipment and insurance costs. Start at that floor and raise prices as your portfolio and reviews grow.
### Which photography business is most profitable?
Niches with repeat business, such as corporate headshots, product photography and real estate, tend to give steadier income. Weddings pay well per event but need constant new bookings.
### Do I need a website if I have Instagram?
Yes. Instagram shows your style; a website explains packages, answers questions, ranks in local search and captures inquiries you can follow up.
:::
