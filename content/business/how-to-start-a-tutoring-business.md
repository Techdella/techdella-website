To start a tutoring business, choose subjects and exam levels where you can show results, put safeguarding in place, register for tax, price sessions in termly or monthly blocks, and build referrals from parents and schools. Tutoring has low startup costs and steady demand around exams. The hard part is filling a timetable that clusters after school and on weekends, which is why small groups and online sessions matter.

This guide covers how to start a tutoring business in the UK, the US and Nigeria, with safeguarding, startup costs, pricing and marketing.

:::takeaways
- Specialize by exam and level (GCSE maths, SAT, WAEC and JAMB prep). Parents search for specific outcomes.
- Safeguarding is part of the product. Parents expect checks, policies and session records.
- Sell blocks of sessions per term or month. It steadies income and improves results.
- Small groups raise your hourly income and make after-school hours go further.
:::

## Which tutoring niche should you choose?

Define your [target audience](/glossary/target-audience/) by subject, level and outcome.

| Niche | Who pays | Peak season | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Exam prep (GCSE, A level, SAT, WAEC, JAMB) | Parents | Months before exams | 1:1 and small groups | Results-driven; strong referrals |
| Primary and entrance exams (11+, common entrance) | Parents | Autumn to winter | 1:1 | High stakes, high rates |
| Special educational needs support | Parents, sometimes schools | Year-round | 1:1 | Needs specialist training |
| Adult skills (languages, coding, professional exams) | Adults, employers | Year-round | Online, groups | Daytime slots available |
| School partnerships | Schools | Term-time | Groups | Contracts and safeguarding paperwork |

Adult learners and international students fill daytime hours, which pairs well with after-school exam tutoring.

## What do you need to start tutoring legally and safely?

**United Kingdom.** A basic DBS check costs £20 and anyone 16 or over working in England and Wales can apply ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/request-copy-criminal-record)). Many parents and agencies expect it. Register as a sole trader once self-employment income passes £1,000 in a tax year ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-sole-trader)). Because you hold children's data, check whether you need to pay the [ICO data protection fee](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/).

**United States.** There is usually no tutoring licence, but many families and schools ask for a background check. Register as a sole proprietor or LLC ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/register-your-business)) and get a [free EIN](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number). If you run a learning center, check local zoning and business licences.

**Nigeria.** Register a business name with the [CAC](https://www.cac.gov.ng/). If you open a learning center rather than tutoring at home or online, check state education ministry requirements for private centers.

**Safeguarding everywhere.** Write a simple policy: sessions in shared spaces or with a parent nearby, online sessions on platforms parents can see, no private messaging with students outside agreed channels, and records of each session.

## How should you price tutoring?

Set an hourly rate for one-to-one, a per-student rate for small groups, and sell both in blocks. Worked example (illustrative), UK GCSE maths tutor:

- One-to-one: £40 an hour. Prep and marking: 15 minutes per session.
- Effective rate: £40 ÷ 1.25 hours = **£32 an hour** of your time.
- Small group of 4: £18 per student → 4 × £18 = £72 a session. With 30 minutes of prep, effective rate = £72 ÷ 1.5 = **£48 an hour**.
- A term block of 10 sessions per student, paid upfront.

Weekly timetable: 12 one-to-one sessions (12 × £40 = £480) plus 4 group sessions (4 × £72 = £288) = **£768 a week**. Over 38 teaching weeks, that is 38 × £768 = **£29,184** before costs and tax, from about 21 hours of contact and prep a week (12 × 1.25 + 4 × 1.5).

In Nigeria, many tutors charge monthly for a fixed number of sessions per week, with higher rates in the months before WAEC and JAMB. Online tutoring for students abroad can be priced in their currency.

## How to start a tutoring business: step by step

:::steps "Fill your first term"
1. Choose subjects and levels | Where you have strong knowledge and, ideally, results to show.
2. Put safeguarding in place | Background check, written policy and a session record template.
3. Register and set up payments | Tax registration, business account and an easy payment method for parents.
4. Build your materials | Diagnostic test, lesson plans, homework sets and progress reports.
5. Set prices and blocks | One-to-one, small-group and term-block pricing.
6. Get your first five students | Friends, former colleagues, schools, community and religious groups.
7. Report progress | Send parents a short update every few weeks. It drives renewals and referrals.
:::

## How do tutors find students?

- **Parent referrals.** Most tutoring is bought on recommendation. A small [referral marketing](/glossary/referral-marketing/) reward, like a free session, helps.
- **Local search.** "Maths tutor near me" or "JAMB tutor in Ibadan" are high-intent searches. A Google Business Profile and a site with subject and location pages help; see our [local SEO service](/services/local-seo/).
- **Schools and community groups.** Offer exam workshops or revision sessions.

:::myth-fact
Myth: You need a teaching qualification to tutor.
Fact: There is no general legal requirement in the UK, US or Nigeria. Parents look for subject expertise, results, safeguarding and clear progress updates.
Myth: Tutoring income stops in the holidays.
Fact: Holiday revision courses, adult learners and international online students can fill the gaps. Plan them before the school year ends.
:::

:::faq
### How much does it cost to start a tutoring business?
Very little if you tutor online or at students' homes: a laptop, materials, a background check and a simple listing. A learning center costs much more because of rent and licensing.
### Do I need a DBS check to tutor?
It is not always a legal requirement for private tutors, but parents and agencies usually expect it. A basic DBS check in England and Wales costs £20.
### How much should a tutor charge?
Base your rate on your subject, level, experience and local market, and count prep time. Small groups let you charge less per student and earn more per hour.
### Is online tutoring as effective as in person?
It can be, especially for older students, with good tools and engaged learners. Many tutors offer both and let parents choose.
### How do I get my first tutoring students?
Tell your network, contact schools and community groups, set up a Google Business Profile and ask early families for reviews and referrals.
:::
