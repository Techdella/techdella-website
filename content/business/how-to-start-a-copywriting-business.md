To start a copywriting business, choose a niche and one or two deliverables you'll be known for (landing pages, email sequences, product descriptions or ad copy), build a small portfolio that shows your thinking, register the business, and price per project. Then send targeted pitches every week until referrals take over.

This guide covers how to start a copywriting business in Nigeria, the US and the UK, with pricing maths, the ad rules your copy must follow, and how to stay valuable when every client has an AI writing tool.

:::takeaways
- Copywriting is mostly a self-employed profession. Your business skills matter as much as your writing.
- Specialise by industry and deliverable, such as "email sequences for SaaS" or "product pages for beauty brands".
- Price by project, from your target hourly rate, and limit revision rounds.
- Your copy must follow advertising law in each market: the FTC in the US, the CAP Code in the UK and ARCON rules in Nigeria.
:::

AI tools have made first drafts cheap. They haven't made it cheap to know which promise will make a specific buyer act, which claim is legally safe, or how a page should be structured to rank and convert. Copywriters who sell those judgments, and can show results, are the ones who keep raising prices.

## Step 1: Pick a niche and a deliverable

A niche makes you easier to find and easier to recommend. Combine an industry with a format:

| Industry | Deliverables that sell well | Why |
|---|---|---|
| SaaS and tech | Landing pages, onboarding emails, case studies | Clear conversion goals and budgets |
| Ecommerce and DTC | Product pages, email flows, ad copy | Direct link between copy and sales |
| Fintech | Website copy, compliance-friendly ads, explainers | Rules are strict, so expertise is valued |
| Professional services | Service pages, LinkedIn content, proposals | Trust-led buying |
| Local businesses | Website copy, Google Business Profile posts | High volume, smaller budgets |

If you've worked in an industry, start there. If not, choose one you'll enjoy reading about every day, because good copy starts with research. Our [copywriting glossary entry](/glossary/copywriting/) covers the core formats.

## Step 2: Build a portfolio that shows thinking

You don't need dozens of samples. Three to five strong pieces in your niche are enough. For each one, show:

1. **The brief:** who the reader was and what they needed to do.
2. **Your reasoning:** the angle you chose and why.
3. **The copy itself.**
4. **The result**, if the client confirmed it and agreed to share it.

No clients yet? Rewrite a real [landing page](/glossary/landing-page/) from a company in your niche and explain your changes. Label it clearly as a spec sample, and never imply the brand hired you.

## Step 3: Register the business and know the ad rules

| | United States | United Kingdom | Nigeria |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structure | Sole proprietorship or LLC ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/choose-business-structure)) | Sole trader (Self Assessment once trading income tops £1,000) or £100 limited company ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-sole-trader)) | Business name or company with the [CAC](https://www.cac.gov.ng/) |
| Tax ID | Free EIN from the [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number) | UTR from HMRC; VAT once turnover passes £90,000 ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration/when-to-register)) | TIN |
| Advertising rules your copy must follow | FTC truth-in-advertising rules; endorsements must disclose material connections ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)) | The CAP Code, enforced by the ASA; ads must be obviously identifiable ([ASA](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/recognising-ads-social-media.html)) | ARCON requires pre-exposure approval of adverts, including sponsored content online ([ARCON](https://arcon.gov.ng/)) |

:::callout tip "Put claim checks in your process"
Ask clients to supply evidence for every number and claim ("50% faster", "trusted by 1,000 firms"). Keep it on file. If they can't prove it, rewrite the line. It protects them, and it protects your reputation.
:::

Your contract should also say who owns the copy. Most clients expect full rights once they've paid in full. Keep the right to show the work in your portfolio unless you agree otherwise.

## Step 4: Set your prices

**Worked example (replace with your numbers):**

- Target income: $5,000 a month before tax. Tools, insurance and admin costs: $250 a month. You need **$5,250**.
- You can bill about 70 hours a month. The rest is pitching, admin and learning.
- $5,250 ÷ 70 = **$75 per billable hour**.
- A landing page takes 2 hours of research, 3 hours of writing and 1 hour of revisions: 6 hours × $75 = **$450 minimum**.
- A five-email welcome sequence takes about 10 hours: **$750 minimum**.

Quote the project price, not the hours. As you get faster and your results improve, the price stays the same and your effective rate climbs. With clients in Nigeria, local budgets are often smaller, so consider a lighter package; with US and UK clients, price in their currency.

:::myth-fact
Myth: AI will replace copywriters, so there's no point starting.
Fact: AI produces drafts. Clients still need someone to understand the buyer, pick the angle, check the claims and take responsibility for the result. That's the service to sell.
Myth: Charging per word is the professional standard.
Fact: Per-word pricing rewards length, not results. Project pricing ties your fee to the outcome the page or email should deliver.
:::

## Where AI fits in a copywriting business

Use AI tools the way a good editor uses an assistant: for research summaries, outline options, headline variations and first-pass edits. Don't hand them the parts clients pay you for: understanding the buyer, choosing the angle and checking every claim against evidence.

Be open with clients about how you use these tools, and check your contracts. Some clients ban AI-generated text for confidentiality or originality reasons, and some ask you to confirm that the copy is original. Never paste confidential client material into a tool unless the client has agreed and the tool's data terms allow it.

A useful positioning line: "I use AI to work faster, and I'm responsible for every word you publish." Clients are buying judgment and accountability.

## Step 5: Set up simple systems

- **Intake form** that captures audience, offer, proof, tone and deadlines.
- **Proposal template** with scope, price, two revision rounds and payment terms.
- **Style and claims checklist** you run before every delivery. A [readability checker](/tools/free-readability-checker/) helps keep copy clear.
- **Invoicing** with a 50% deposit for new clients.
- **Swipe file** of good copy in your niche, organised by format.

## Step 6: Find clients fast

:::steps "Your first 60 days"
1. Make a target list | 50 businesses in your niche with weak copy on a key page.
2. Send personal pitches | Five a day, each with one specific suggestion for their page.
3. Partner with agencies | Design, SEO and ad agencies often need reliable writers for client work.
4. Publish your expertise | Post teardowns of copy in your niche on LinkedIn each week.
5. Turn projects into retainers | Offer a monthly package of emails or pages after every successful project.
6. Ask for referrals | When a client is happy, ask who else they know who needs help.
:::

Agencies are a strong early channel. SEO teams in particular need writers who can write for people and search engines at the same time; learn the basics of [SEO](/services/seo/) and [email marketing](/services/email-marketing/) and you become easier to hire.

## Copywriting business FAQs

:::faq
### How do I start a copywriting business with no experience?
Pick a niche, study the best copy in it, and write three to five spec samples labelled as samples. Offer a small paid project to a local business or non-profit to get a real result and testimonial, then raise prices as your portfolio grows.
### How much should a beginner copywriter charge?
Work out your target hourly rate from the income you need and the hours you can bill, then multiply by the time each project takes. Quote fixed project prices and cap revisions at two rounds.
### Do I need a website for my copywriting business?
It helps a lot. A simple site with your niche, services, samples and a booking link shows clients you can write persuasive pages, and it lets you rank for searches in your niche.
### Do I need to register a copywriting business?
Yes, once you're earning. Register as a sole trader or company, get a tax ID, and keep business income separate. In the UK, register for Self Assessment once trading income passes £1,000 a year.
### How do I find copywriting clients fast?
Send targeted, personal pitches with a specific suggestion, partner with agencies that need writers, and post short teardowns of copy in your niche. Every finished project is a chance to pitch a retainer or ask for a referral.
:::
