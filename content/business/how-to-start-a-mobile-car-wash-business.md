To start a mobile car wash business, register and insure the business, buy a compact kit built around a water-safe washing method, set clear packages and prices, and build routes of repeat customers in the same neighbourhoods and business parks. The fastest path to steady income is recurring work: weekly or fortnightly home clients and fleet contracts with companies that run vans or cars.

This guide covers how to start a mobile car wash business in the US, the UK and Nigeria, including the wash-water rules many new operators miss.

:::takeaways
- The market is huge: hundreds of millions of vehicles in the US and over 40 million in the UK.
- Don't let dirty water reach storm drains. Waterless, rinseless or water-capture methods keep you compliant and mobile.
- Fleets (vans, company cars, ride-hailing drivers) give volume in one location.
- Memberships and recurring bookings turn one-off washes into predictable income.
:::

## Step 1: Pick your services and customers

| Service | Time per vehicle | Who buys |
|---|---|---|
| Exterior wash | 20–40 minutes | Busy professionals, office car parks |
| Wash and interior vacuum | 45–75 minutes | Families, regular home clients |
| Full valet or detail | 2–5 hours | Car enthusiasts, sellers preparing a car |
| Fleet wash | 15–30 minutes per van | Trades, delivery and rental companies |
| Add-ons | Varies | Clay bar, wax, upholstery shampoo, headlight restoration |

Start with two packages and one or two add-ons. Detailing earns more per hour but takes skill; basic washes build volume and recurring routes.

## Step 2: Choose a water-safe method

This is the step most new operators skip, and it can lead to fines and complaints.

| | United States | United Kingdom | Nigeria |
|---|---|---|---|
| The rule | Cities must prohibit non-stormwater discharges to storm sewers under Clean Water Act stormwater permits, and many require mobile washers to contain and collect wash water (for example, [Portland's guidance](https://www.portland.gov/bes/preventing-pollution/prevent-pollution/vehicle-and-equipment-washing)). Some cities require a permit for mobile washing. | Run-off from vehicle cleaning must not enter surface water drains, surface waters or groundwater. Discharging trade effluent to a foul sewer needs the water company's consent ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/discharges-to-surface-water-and-groundwater-environmental-permits)). | State environmental protection agencies regulate pollution and drainage; ask yours about wash-water disposal, especially in estates and business parks |
| Practical approach | Waterless or rinseless products, drain covers, and water-capture mats with a recovery pump | The same, plus permission from car-park owners | Rinseless methods save water where supply is limited; get estate or car-park permission |

:::callout tip "Use rinseless methods as your default"
Rinseless and waterless products clean most lightly soiled cars with a few litres of water and no run-off. Keep a capture mat and pump for heavily soiled vehicles. It's also faster, because there's nothing to hose down.
:::

## Step 5: Price packages with route maths

**Worked example (replace with your numbers):**

- Wash and interior vacuum: $45, taking 60 minutes including setup.
- Chemicals and towels per car: $3. So each wash contributes **$42**.
- If customers are spread out, you lose 30 minutes driving between jobs: 6 cars a day, **$252**.
- If you cluster clients in one apartment complex or office park, travel drops to 5 minutes: 8 cars a day, **$336**.
- A fleet contract for 20 vans at $22 each, 20 minutes per van: about 7 hours on one site for **$440** a visit (less chemicals).

Clustering turns the same hours into a third more revenue, and fleets add volume without travel. That's why the best mobile car wash businesses sell by location.

:::myth-fact
Myth: A mobile car wash needs a big water tank and a pressure washer.
Fact: Rinseless and waterless methods clean most cars with minimal water and no run-off, which is cheaper, faster and easier to keep compliant.
Myth: One-off washes are the main business.
Fact: Recurring weekly or fortnightly clients and fleet contracts provide steady income. Memberships raise [customer lifetime value](/glossary/customer-lifetime-value/).
:::

### Turn one-off washes into memberships

A membership is a fixed monthly price for a set number of washes, booked on a regular schedule. It smooths your income through quiet weeks and makes route planning easier, because you know where you'll be.

A simple structure: a fortnightly exterior wash for one price, a fortnightly wash and interior for a higher price, and a quarterly full valet as an add-on. Take payment by card or direct debit monthly, set a minimum term of three months, and let members skip a visit with notice. Offer households with two cars a second-vehicle discount; it's another car at the same stop.

## Mobile car wash business FAQs

:::faq
### How much does it cost to start a mobile car wash business?
If you use your own vehicle and a rinseless kit, you can start with a few hundred to a few thousand dollars or pounds for supplies, insurance and a booking website. A used van, tank and detailing tools raise that substantially.
### Do I need a permit for a mobile car wash?
You need to register the business and may need a local business licence. Some US cities require a mobile washing permit, and both US and UK rules prohibit dirty wash water entering storm or surface drains. Always get the property owner's permission.
### Do I need a physical shop?
No. That's the point of a mobile business. You need a vehicle, a compliant washing method, a booking system and somewhere to store supplies.
### How do I get customers for my mobile car wash?
Focus on dense areas like apartment complexes and office parks, set up a Google Business Profile, offer memberships, and pitch fleet operators who need many vehicles cleaned in one place.
### Is a waterless car wash safe for paint?
Quality waterless and rinseless products with plenty of clean microfibre towels are designed for lightly to moderately soiled cars. Heavily muddy vehicles need a pre-rinse with water capture first.
:::
