To start a digital marketing business, specialize in one service for one type of client, register the business, put a short contract in place, price monthly retainers from your real hours, and prove results with a few pilot clients before you scale. You can start from a laptop. What separates a business from a side gig is a repeatable offer, clean reporting and a pipeline that doesn't depend on favors.

This guide covers how to start a digital marketing business in the US, the UK and Nigeria, with registration steps, compliance basics, pricing maths and how to get clients.

:::takeaways
- Specialize. "SEO for dental clinics" sells faster than "full-service digital marketing".
- Clients should own their ad accounts, analytics and domains. You get access, never ownership.
- Retainers are your income base. Price them from hours, tools and a profit margin, not from guesswork.
- Data protection rules (GDPR in the UK, NDPA in Nigeria, CAN-SPAM in the US) apply to you as soon as you handle client contact lists.
:::

## How do you register a digital marketing business?

**United States.** Choose a sole proprietorship or LLC and register with your state ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/register-your-business)). Get a [free EIN](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number). If you send marketing emails for clients, the FTC's CAN-SPAM rules require a valid postal address in each message and honoring opt-outs within 10 business days, with penalties of up to $53,088 per violating email ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)).

**United Kingdom.** Register as a sole trader once self-employment income passes £1,000 in a tax year ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-sole-trader)) or form a limited company for £100 ([Companies House](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company)). Organizations that use personal information, including sole traders, must pay the [ICO data protection fee](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/) unless exempt. Register for VAT if taxable turnover passes £90,000 in 12 months ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration/when-to-register)).

**Nigeria.** Register a business name or limited company with the [CAC](https://www.cac.gov.ng/). Personal data you handle for clients falls under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, overseen by the [Nigeria Data Protection Commission](https://ndpc.gov.ng/). Many Nigerian agencies bill foreign clients in dollars, so set up a compliant way to receive them early.

## How should you price a marketing retainer?

Work it out from time, not from what a big agency charges. Worked example (illustrative):

- Monthly SEO retainer for a local business: 20 hours of your time.
- Your target rate: $60 an hour → 20 × $60 = $1,200.
- Tools and reporting software allocated to this client: $100.
- Markup for profit and slow months: 20% of $1,300 = $260.
- Retainer: $1,200 + $100 + $260 = **$1,560**, rounded to **$1,500–$1,600**.

Six clients at $1,500 is 6 × $1,500 × 12 = **$108,000** in yearly revenue, using about 120 client hours a month. That leaves room for sales and admin. Ten clients at that rate would need help: a contractor or your first hire.

For paid ads, many agencies charge a monthly fee with a minimum, or a percentage of ad spend. Write the minimum in the contract so small budgets still cover your time.

Taxes come off the top: US self-employment tax of 15.3% ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employment-tax-social-security-and-medicare-taxes)), UK Class 4 National Insurance at 6% on profits between £12,570 and £50,270 ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/self-employed-national-insurance-rates)), and Nigeria's personal income tax bands (0% on the first ₦800,000, then 15% on the next ₦2,200,000) under the 2025 Act ([Aluko & Oyebode, 2025](https://aluko-oyebode.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Overview-of-the-Notable-Changes-Introduced-by-the-New-Nigeria-Tax-Acts-2025.pdf)).

## How to start a digital marketing business: step by step

:::steps "From freelancer to business in 90 days"
1. Pick one service and one client type | Write it as one line, for example "Google Ads for physiotherapy clinics in Lagos".
2. Run two or three pilot projects | Discount in exchange for permission to publish results and a testimonial.
3. Register and open a business account | Keep client payments and personal money separate.
4. Write your contract | Scope, deliverables, reporting, notice period, who owns accounts and content.
5. Standardize reporting | Use the same monthly report for every client. Tag every link with our [UTM generator](/tools/free-utm-parameter-generator/) so results are traceable.
6. Publish proof | Turn each pilot into a short case study with the numbers the client approved.
7. Build one pipeline | Referrals, LinkedIn outreach, or content. Measure which one books calls.
:::

## How do you get your first digital marketing clients?

Start with businesses you already understand. A marketer who worked in hospitality sells to restaurants with less effort than one starting cold.

- **Warm network first.** Former employers, suppliers and friends who run businesses.
- **Free audits as a door-opener.** A short, specific audit (five issues, ranked) shows skill without giving away the work.
- **Partnerships.** Web designers, accountants and business coaches meet small businesses that need marketing every week.
- **Show your own results.** Rank your own site, run your own ads, grow your own list. Clients notice.

When you outgrow solo delivery, study how established teams scope [SEO](/services/seo/), [paid ads](/services/paid-ads/) and [email marketing](/services/email-marketing/) as separate, measurable services.

:::myth-fact
Myth: You need to offer every channel to compete with agencies.
Fact: Specialists usually win small and mid-size clients because they can show results in one channel quickly. Add services once you have proof.
Myth: Running ads from your own account is easier for the client.
Fact: It creates risk for both sides. The client should own the ad account, analytics and pixels; you work as a user with access. It protects their data and your reputation.
:::

:::faq
### Can I start a digital marketing business with no experience?
Yes, but get experience before you sell retainers. Run campaigns for your own projects, then two or three discounted pilots. Paying clients want proof you can deliver, not certificates alone.
### How much does it cost to start a digital marketing business?
Often under $1,000 or £800 if you already have a laptop: registration, a few tools and a simple website. Costs rise with paid software, insurance and contractors.
### What is the most profitable digital marketing service?
Services that are recurring and tied to revenue, such as paid ads management, SEO and email for ecommerce, tend to support higher retainers. Profit depends on your delivery efficiency more than the service itself.
### Do I need a license to run a marketing agency?
No specific licence is needed in the US, UK or Nigeria. You do need to register the business for tax, follow data protection and email marketing rules, and follow each ad platform's advertising policies.
### How many clients do I need?
Divide your target yearly revenue by your average retainer times 12. At $1,500 a month, six clients bring in $108,000 a year. Plan your delivery hours before you take the seventh.
:::
