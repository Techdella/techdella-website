## Why readability matters

People scan web pages. Long sentences and long words slow them down, and slow readers leave. Clear copy also helps search engines and AI answer engines pull a direct answer from your page.

Readability formulas are rough. They count sentence length and syllables, not meaning. Use the score to spot problems, then use your judgment.

## A worked example

Before: "Our comprehensive, end-to-end solution enables organizations to optimize their marketing operations through the strategic utilization of automation technologies."

That's one 19-word sentence full of long words, roughly a college-graduate reading level.

After: "We set up marketing automation for you. Your team spends less time on manual work."

Two short sentences that say the same thing, at a much lower grade.

## Quick fixes

- Keep most sentences under 20 words.
- One idea per sentence.
- Use "you" and "we".
- Swap "utilize" for "use", "in order to" for "to".
