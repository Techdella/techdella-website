## Why build a persona

A persona puts one real buyer at the center of your marketing. Instead of writing for "businesses", you write for the finance manager who's tired of chasing invoices in spreadsheets. Messages get sharper, and channel choices get easier.

## A worked example

Input: **invoicing software for logistics companies**, buyer: **finance manager**, size: **51–200**, market: **US**.

A useful draft persona would cover goals (close the month faster), pains (manual reconciliation, late payments), objections (switching cost, integration with the current accounting tool), channels (LinkedIn, peer communities, review sites) and message angles to test ("Close the month two days sooner").

Your output will differ. Then do the important part: take it to real calls and mark what holds up.

## Turning a persona into action

- Use the pains as headlines on your landing pages.
- Use the objections as FAQ entries and sales-call prep.
- Use the channels to decide where to test ads first.
