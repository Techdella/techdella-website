## Why the subject line matters

The subject line decides whether an email gets opened, ignored or reported. It has to say what's inside, fast, on a small screen, next to dozens of other emails.

## A worked example

Before: **"🚀 HUGE NEWS!!! Don't miss our AMAZING new features"**

Too long for mobile, all caps, triple exclamation marks and vague. The reader can't tell what's new.

After: **"Your audit report now shows AI search visibility"**

Specific, under 50 characters, and it says what the reader gets.

## What good looks like

- Say what's inside, not how exciting it is.
- Front-load the important words.
- Use the preview text to add the second half of the message.
- Keep authentication in order: no subject line fixes poor deliverability.
