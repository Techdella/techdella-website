## Why on-page SEO still matters

On-page SEO is the part you fully control. A clear title, one H1, a useful meta description, descriptive alt text and sensible links help search engines and AI answer engines understand what a page is about. Getting these right won't win a competitive query on its own, but getting them wrong can hold a good page back.

## A worked example

A typical service page might come back with three Fix items:

1. **Two H1 tags.** The theme printed the logo as an H1 as well as the page title. The fix is to make the logo a plain link.
2. **No meta description.** Search engines pull a cookie notice as the snippet. The fix is a 150-character description written for the click.
3. **Images with no alt text.** The fix is a short description of what each image shows.

## What the checker can't see

- Whether your content is the best answer for the query.
- Backlinks and brand mentions.
- Page speed in the field (use PageSpeed Insights for that).
