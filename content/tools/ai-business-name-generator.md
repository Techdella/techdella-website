## Why the name matters

A name is the first thing people hear about you. A good one is easy to say, easy to remember and easy to find online. It doesn't need to describe everything you do.

## A worked example

Brief: **cross-border payments for freelancers**, words liked: **flow, bridge, pay**, tone: **modern**.

You'll get a mix of styles: invented words, compounds of the words you gave, descriptive names and metaphors. We'd shortlist three, say each out loud, and ask five people to spell them after hearing them once.

## Before you choose

- Search the name with your industry to see who already uses it.
- Check the trademark register in each market you sell in.
- Check the .com (or your local domain) and the main social handles.
- Avoid names that only work in one language if you sell across markets.
