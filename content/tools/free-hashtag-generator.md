## Why hashtags still matter

Hashtags work as labels. They help a platform decide who might want to see a post, and they make a post findable for people who follow or search a tag. A few precise tags beat a long list of broad ones.

## A worked example

Topic: **fintech product launch in Lagos**, platform: **LinkedIn**.

The generator builds tags from your own words (`#FintechProductLaunch`, `#Lagos`) and adds related ones from its fintech, startup and Africa lists (`#Fintech`, `#LagosStartups`). For LinkedIn it keeps the set to about five. Pick the three to five that describe the post best.

## How we use them

- One tag for the topic, one for the audience, one for the place or event.
- Put them at the end of the post, not in the middle of sentences.
- Reuse a branded tag across a series so people can follow it.
