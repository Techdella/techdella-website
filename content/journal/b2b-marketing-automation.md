B2B marketing automation is the use of software to run the repeatable parts of marketing and the handoff to sales: capturing leads, enriching and scoring them, routing them to the right person, sending nurture that fits their stage, and syncing what happens next back into your CRM and ad platforms. Done well, it makes follow-up faster and more relevant. Done badly, it sends more email to the wrong people.

The good news is that a handful of workflows do most of the work. The rest is discipline: clean data, clear ownership, and a human in the loop wherever a buyer will see the result.

:::takeaways
- Automate the handoff first. Fast, accurate lead routing usually beats any nurture sequence.
- Clean data is the foundation. Automation multiplies whatever is in your CRM, good or bad.
- Score leads on fit and behavior, and let scores decay when people go quiet.
- Use AI agents for research, drafting and triage. Keep a human approving anything a prospect sees.
- Measure automation by response time, qualified leads and pipeline, not by emails sent.
:::

## What B2B marketing automation should do

[Marketing automation](marketing:/glossary/marketing-automation/) in B2B has four jobs. If a workflow doesn't serve one of them, question whether you need it.

1. **Respond fast.** Get the right lead to the right person, with context, while interest is high.
2. **Keep data clean.** Deduplicate, enrich and standardize records so every other workflow works.
3. **Nurture by stage.** Send people what fits where they are, and stop when they've had enough.
4. **Close the loop.** Feed outcomes back to marketing reports and ad platforms so spend follows pipeline.

## The ten B2B marketing automation workflows worth running

Ranked roughly by impact for a typical B2B team. Start at the top.

| Workflow | Trigger | What it does | Owner | Measure it by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Speed-to-lead routing | Demo, audit or contact form | Creates or updates the contact, assigns an owner, alerts them with context, creates a deal | RevOps | Time to first response |
| 2. Dedupe and enrichment | Any new contact | Merges duplicates, adds company size, industry and role | RevOps | Duplicate rate; field completeness |
| 3. Lead scoring | Fit data plus activity | Adds fit and behavior points, decays inactivity, flags MQLs | Marketing and sales together | MQL-to-SQL rate |
| 4. High-intent alerts | Pricing page visit, second visit to a service page, reply | Notifies the account owner in real time | Sales | Meetings booked from alerts |
| 5. Stage-based nurture | Lead magnet download, webinar signup | Sends a short sequence matched to topic and stage | Marketing | Reply and meeting rate |
| 6. Event and webinar follow-up | Attended or missed | Sends the recording, a next step and a relevant resource | Marketing | Post-event meetings |
| 7. Recycle from sales | Deal closed-lost or stalled | Returns contact to nurture with a reason code and a re-check date | Sales and RevOps | Recycled leads that reopen |
| 8. Re-engagement and sunset | No opens or clicks for a set period | One re-permission email, then suppress | Marketing | Spam rate; list health |
| 9. Offline conversion sync | Lead qualifies, opportunity created, deal won | Sends outcomes to Google, LinkedIn and Meta | RevOps | Ad cost per qualified lead |
| 10. Customer onboarding and expansion | Deal won | Welcome sequence, check-ins, usage tips, referral and review asks | Customer success | Retention; [customer lifetime value](marketing:/glossary/customer-lifetime-value/) |

Workflow 8 matters more than it looks. Mailing people who stopped engaging raises spam complaints, and Gmail and Yahoo enforce limits on those. Our [B2B email deliverability guide](/b2b-email-deliverability/) explains the rules.

## How to score and route leads

Lead scoring adds up two kinds of evidence: **fit** (is this the kind of company and person we sell to?) and **behavior** (are they showing buying intent?). Keep it simple enough that sales can explain any score.

A worked example with an illustrative model. MQL threshold: 60 points or more.

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| Role: head of marketing, growth or revenue | +20 |
| Company size 50–500 employees | +15 |
| Target industry | +15 |
| Visited the pricing page | +15 |
| Downloaded a guide | +5 |
| Requested an audit or demo | +40 |
| No activity for 30 days | −10 |

A head of marketing at a 120-person SaaS company who downloads a guide and then visits pricing scores 20 + 15 + 15 + 5 + 15 = **70**. That passes 60, so they become an MQL and the account owner gets an alert with what they read.

The same person a month later with no further activity drops to 70 − 10 = **60**, still an MQL. Another quiet month takes them to **50**, and they move back to nurture. The decay rule stops old interest from clogging the sales queue.

Two rules keep scoring honest:

- **Any direct request skips the queue.** A demo or audit request goes straight to a person, whatever the score.
- **Review the model quarterly with sales.** Look at which scores became opportunities and adjust the points.

## Worked example: what speed-to-lead routing saves

Here's the arithmetic for workflow 1, with illustrative numbers.

- Inbound leads per month: **160**
- Manual triage per lead (check duplicates, look up the company, assign, notify): **6 minutes**
- Manual time: 160 × 6 = **960 minutes, or 16 hours a month**
- With automated routing and enrichment, a 1-minute human check per lead: 160 × 1 = **160 minutes, or about 2.7 hours**
- Time saved: **about 13.3 hours a month**

The hours matter, but response time matters more. Manual triage happens in batches, so a lead that arrives at 4 p.m. on a Friday can wait until Monday. Automated routing alerts the right person within minutes, with context, while the buyer still has your page open.

## AI agents in B2B marketing: realistic uses

An AI agent is software that uses a language model to plan and take steps toward a goal, often by calling other tools. Vendors now label many features as agents. Gartner has warned about "agent washing", estimating that only about 130 of the thousands of agentic AI vendors are real ([Gartner, 2025](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027)).

Here's how we sort the use cases:

| Use case | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Summarizing a new lead's company and recent news for the account owner | Good fit now | Internal, low risk, saves research time; a person reads it before acting |
| Drafting first versions of nurture emails, ad variations and landing page copy | Good fit with review | Speeds up drafting; a person edits for accuracy, voice and claims |
| Triaging inbound messages and form notes into categories | Good fit with review | Repetitive classification; spot-check a sample weekly |
| Turning call notes into CRM updates and next steps | Good fit with review | Saves admin; the rep confirms before saving |
| Answering prospect questions on your website | Careful | Needs approved sources, clear limits and a handoff to a human |
| Sending personalized outreach to prospects without review | Not yet | Errors and wrong claims reach buyers directly and damage trust |
| Changing budgets, bids or pricing on its own | Not yet | Financial decisions need human approval and audit trails |

The pattern: let agents prepare and draft, let people approve and send. If you want to build custom AI workflows beyond what your marketing platform offers, our Studios team covers [AI automation builds](studios:/services/ai-automation/).

:::checklist "Guardrails before you switch on any AI agent"
- A named owner who reviews its output and can switch it off
- A written list of what it may and may not do
- Approved sources it can draw facts and claims from
- Human approval before anything reaches a prospect or customer
- No access to more data than the task needs
- Logs of what it did, kept and reviewed weekly
- A fallback to a human when it's unsure
- A success metric agreed before launch, checked after 30 days
:::

## A 30-day setup plan

:::steps "Get the foundations right"
1. Map the journey | Draw every path from first touch to closed deal: forms, owners, handoffs, delays.
2. Agree definitions | Lead, MQL and SQL, response-time targets and recycle rules, signed off by sales.
3. Clean the data | Deduplicate, standardize fields, remove role addresses and contacts with no permission.
4. Build routing first | Workflow 1, with alerts and context, then test it with real submissions.
5. Add scoring and alerts | A simple fit-plus-behavior model and real-time alerts for high-intent actions.
6. Launch one nurture | One stage, one topic, three to five emails, with a clear exit.
7. Close the loop | Sync qualified and won outcomes to your ad platforms and reports.
:::

:::faq
### What is B2B marketing automation?
B2B marketing automation is software that runs repeatable marketing and sales-handoff tasks: capturing, enriching, scoring and routing leads, sending nurture by stage, alerting sales to buying signals and syncing outcomes back to your CRM and ad platforms. Its goal is faster, more relevant follow-up and clearer reporting on pipeline.
### What should a B2B company automate first?
Speed-to-lead routing and data cleanup. Make sure every demo or contact request reaches the right person with context within minutes, and that new records are deduplicated and enriched. Nurture sequences and lead scoring come next, once the foundation is reliable.
### How does lead scoring work in B2B?
Lead scoring adds points for fit, such as role, company size and industry, and for behavior, such as pricing page visits or downloads. When a lead passes a threshold, it becomes a marketing-qualified lead and goes to sales. Points should decay with inactivity, and direct requests should bypass scoring entirely.
### Are AI agents useful for B2B marketing?
Yes, for bounded tasks with human review: researching accounts, drafting emails and ads, classifying inbound messages and turning call notes into CRM updates. Avoid letting agents send to prospects or change budgets without approval. Gartner predicts over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027.
### How do I measure marketing automation?
Measure outcomes, not activity: time to first response, MQL-to-SQL rate, meetings from alerts, pipeline influenced by nurture, and list health measures like spam complaint rate. Emails sent and workflows built are inputs, not results.
### Which marketing automation platform is best for B2B?
The best one is the one your team will keep clean and use. Start from your process and your CRM: if your CRM includes automation, use it before adding another tool. Check native CRM sync, form handling, lead scoring, reporting and how easily you can send outcomes to ad platforms.
:::
