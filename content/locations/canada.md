## A B2B marketing agency for Canadian companies

Techdella is a B2B marketing agency that runs search, paid ads, lead generation, email and AI marketing automation for Canadian companies. We work remotely and plan every campaign around Canada’s consent rules, its two official languages and a market that sits next to a much larger one.

## Competing next to the US

Canadian buyers often see US vendors first in search results and ads. To win, your site has to answer Canadian questions directly: prices in CAD, where data is stored, whether you support French, and who your Canadian customers are. We write those answers into your service and comparison pages, and we target Canadian search and LinkedIn audiences precisely so budget is not spent on US clicks you cannot serve.

## CASL changes how you build a list

Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation applies to commercial emails and texts sent to or from Canada. You need express or implied consent, your identity in every message and a working unsubscribe. Implied consent expires, two years after a purchase and six months after an inquiry, so consent has to be captured and recorded, not assumed.

We set up forms with clear consent wording, keep consent records in your CRM and plan campaigns that ask for express consent before implied consent runs out. Personal data is handled with PIPEDA in mind, plus Law 25 for Quebec residents.

## French where it counts

If Quebec matters to you, we plan French versions of your key pages, ads and emails and have a native French speaker check them before launch, so they read as written for Quebec rather than translated for it.
