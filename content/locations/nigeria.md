## A marketing agency in Nigeria, founded and based here

Techdella is a marketing agency in Nigeria, founded by Joanna Okedara-Kalu and registered in Ibadan, Oyo State. We run search, paid ads, lead generation, email and AI marketing automation as one connected system for Nigerian companies. We price in naira, work in WAT and plan for how buyers here decide: on a phone, often on WhatsApp, after a close look at who you are.

Search is where we usually start. Buyers here research before they reach out, even when the final decision happens in a meeting or on a call.

## What is different about marketing to Nigerian buyers?

### Trust is checked first

Nigerian buyers check trust quickly. Before they fill in a form, they look for a real team, real clients and a way to talk to a person. That is why we put proof, a physical address and a clear next step near the top of every page.

### Mobile comes first, then WhatsApp

Most of that research happens on a phone. StatCounter puts mobile at 75.22% of web traffic in Nigeria in September 2026. We design pages for mobile data and small screens first, then plan what happens after the click. For many buyers, that means a WhatsApp conversation before an email ever lands.

### Google is the search engine that matters

StatCounter also shows Google with 98.28% of search engine use in Nigeria in September 2026. So we optimize for Google first, and make sure your facts are clear enough for AI answers to pick up as well.

### Checkout and booking must be fast

Payments are not usually the blocker. Nigerian buyers are used to fast transfers and card checkouts, so a slow or confusing checkout or booking flow costs more trust here than it might elsewhere.

### Every city is its own auction

Lagos holds many of the country's banks, fintechs and startup head offices, which means more advertisers bidding for the same buyers. Abuja, Ibadan and Port Harcourt each behave differently. If your product works nationwide, we test cities against each other early, so budget follows results rather than habit. Google and Meta let us target by city or named area, and we split campaigns when the offer or delivery zone changes.

## Compliance we build in

Your forms and lists follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, enforced by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Ads that may need ARCON vetting are flagged before launch, not after. We are not lawyers, so for a formal compliance review we point you to a data protection professional.
