## A marketing agency for Dubai and UAE companies

Techdella is a marketing agency that runs search, paid ads, lead generation, email and AI marketing automation for companies in Dubai and across the UAE. We work remotely and plan for the things that make this market different: two languages, a conversation that usually moves to WhatsApp, and a buyer base that includes both Emiratis and a large international community.

## Two languages, two sets of campaigns

UAE buyers search, read and reply in Arabic, English or both. A single translated campaign usually underperforms in both languages. We plan Arabic and English keywords, ads and landing pages separately, with Arabic pages built right-to-left and checked by a native speaker.

## WhatsApp is the follow-up channel

In the UAE, a lead often wants to continue on WhatsApp rather than wait for an email. We design click-to-WhatsApp ads, opt-in wording and reply templates, and connect them to your CRM so no conversation is lost when a team member is away.

## Data protection in the UAE

Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (the PDPL) covers most UAE companies. Businesses in the DIFC and ADGM free zones follow their own data protection laws. We build forms, lists and tracking around clear consent and easy opt-outs, and we flag where your setup needs a lawyer’s review.

For creator campaigns, influencers and content creators promoting products in the UAE need an advertiser permit from the UAE Media Council. We check permits before any paid partnership.

## Timing that fits your week

Our 9am–6pm WAT day is 12pm–9pm in Dubai, so we overlap your afternoon. We schedule around the Monday-to-Friday week and adjust campaigns for Ramadan. The [Growth Audit](/growth-audit/) is $1,500 and gives you a prioritized plan to start.
