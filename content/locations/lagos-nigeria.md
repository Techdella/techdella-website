## A marketing agency for Lagos teams, working remotely

Techdella is a marketing agency serving companies in Lagos remotely, across the Island and the Mainland. We run search, paid ads, lead generation, email and AI marketing automation as one system, planned for the way Lagos buyers find, check and contact a supplier.

## Lagos is its own market

Lagos is not a smaller version of Nigeria. It is where many of the country’s banks, fintechs, media companies and startups have their head offices, from Victoria Island and Ikoyi to Lekki, Ikeja and the Yaba tech cluster. That means your buyers see more competitors, more ads and more cold outreach than buyers in most other Nigerian cities.

It also means geography shapes the funnel. A buyer in Lekki and a buyer in Ikeja may want different offers, delivery times or meeting formats. Traffic between the Island and the Mainland makes a “quick in-person meeting” costly, so many decisions move to WhatsApp and video calls. We build for that: area-specific landing pages where they make sense, click-to-WhatsApp campaigns, and follow-up that replies within the working day.

## Getting found in a crowded city

For service businesses with a real Lagos address, local search matters. We set up and maintain your Google Business Profile, build pages for the areas you actually serve and keep your name, address and phone details consistent everywhere.

For B2B companies, we focus on the roles you sell to rather than the city name alone: the head of operations at a Lagos bank searches differently from a startup founder in Yaba.
