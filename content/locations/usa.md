## A B2B marketing agency for US companies, working remotely

Techdella is a B2B marketing agency that runs search, paid ads, lead generation, email and AI marketing automation for US companies. Search is our lead offer: we make your company easy to find and easy to choose when a buyer compares vendors on Google or asks an AI assistant for a shortlist.

## What US buyers expect

US B2B buyers do most of their research before they contact sales. They read comparison pages, check reviews and look for pricing. If your site hides the answer, a competitor’s page gives it to them. We write answer-first service pages, honest comparison content and clear pricing or “ways to start” pages so you are part of that shortlist.

The US is also one of the most expensive ad markets for B2B keywords. We spend where intent is highest, measure leads in your CRM rather than in clicks, and cut what does not produce pipeline.

## Privacy and email rules we build in

Email follows CAN-SPAM. Forms and tracking reflect state privacy laws, starting with the CCPA as amended by the CPRA in California and the growing list of similar state laws. SMS only goes to people who gave TCPA-compliant written consent.

## Working across the time difference

We work in West Africa Time, five hours ahead of US Eastern in summer and six in winter. Our afternoon is your morning, which gives East Coast teams a daily window for calls, and work we finish before you start the day is ready for review when you log on. West Coast teams usually meet us early in their morning.
