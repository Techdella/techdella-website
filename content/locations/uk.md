## A B2B marketing agency for UK companies

Techdella is a B2B marketing agency that runs search, paid ads, lead generation, email and AI marketing automation for UK companies. We work remotely, in a time zone that matches the UK for half the year and sits one hour ahead for the rest.

## What UK B2B buyers respond to

UK buyers tend to distrust overstated claims. Specific proof, plain language and transparent pricing do more than big promises. We write that way, and we keep ad copy within the CAP Code so claims hold up if a competitor challenges them with the Advertising Standards Authority.

LinkedIn is where most UK B2B relationships start. We pair founder and team posts with targeted ads for named accounts, then capture active demand with Google Search and SEO pages that answer the questions buyers type.

## UK GDPR and PECR, built in

UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 govern the personal data you collect. PECR adds rules for email and cookies: individual subscribers need consent or the soft opt-in, while corporate addresses can be emailed with a clear identity and opt-out. Non-essential cookies need consent, which changes what your analytics and ad platforms can see.

We set up consent banners, Google Consent Mode v2 and email lists so each lead is collected in a way you can explain to the ICO. That protects your sender reputation as well as your compliance.
