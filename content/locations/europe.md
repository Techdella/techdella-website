## A B2B marketing agency for companies across Europe

Techdella is a B2B marketing agency that runs search, paid ads, lead generation, email and AI marketing automation for companies across Europe. We work remotely, in a time zone that matches Central European Time for half the year, and we plan every campaign around European consent rules and languages.

## Europe is many markets

A buyer in Munich, one in Amsterdam and one in Warsaw search in different languages, trust different signals and expect different levels of formality. We plan campaigns country by country: separate keywords, ads and landing pages per language, checked by native speakers, rather than one English campaign translated several times. We usually recommend starting with one or two priority markets and expanding once those produce qualified leads.

## Consent shapes the whole funnel

The EU GDPR governs the personal data you collect. The ePrivacy Directive, implemented in each country’s own law, adds rules for cookies and marketing email. In practice, that means:

- non-essential cookies, including analytics and ad tracking, wait for consent;
- marketing email to individuals usually needs prior consent, which we collect with double opt-in;
- Google Consent Mode v2 is set up so your ads and analytics keep working with the data people agree to share.

Under the Digital Services Act, ads also cannot be targeted using sensitive personal data or shown to minors based on profiling. We keep targeting simple and write it down.
