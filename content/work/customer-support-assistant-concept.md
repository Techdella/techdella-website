This support assistant concept shows an AI agent connected to orders and CRM, with a person always one message away. See our [AI automation](/services/ai-automation/) service, more [work](/work/), or [start a project](/start/).

## The idea

Most support questions are about an order, a booking or an invoice. This concept answers those from your own data and hands everything else to a person, with the conversation attached.

## How we'd scope it

Start with the three most common questions, connect read-only to orders and CRM, log every answer for review, and add a clear "talk to a person" path from the first message.
