## The brief

Nigerian businesses run payroll, invoices, tax and expenses across spreadsheets, bank apps and WhatsApp. Paycape set out to put all of it in one workspace built around Nigerian compliance: PAYE, VAT, WHT, pension and the monthly tax calendar.

## What we built

TechDella Studios took Paycape from idea to a live product:

- **MVP:** scoped the first release around the jobs owners do every month, so the product launched with the core flows working end to end.
- **UI/UX, end to end:** product flows, wireframes and the full interface, from sign-up and onboarding to payments, invoicing, payroll, tax and reports.
- **Build:** the product itself and the paycape.com website, on desktop and mobile.

Paycape is now live in public beta at [paycape.com](https://paycape.com/). Our marketing team also ran its search growth: read the [Paycape case study](marketing:/case-studies/paycape/).
