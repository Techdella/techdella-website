## The brief

First-time sellers in Nigeria lose time and money on the basics: setting up a store, finding reliable suppliers, taking payments and handling WhatsApp orders. Shopinbos set out to make launching and running an online store simple for them.

## What we built

TechDella Studios built Shopinbos from the first version up:

- **MVP:** scoped the first release around getting a seller from sign-up to a working store, fast.
- **UI/UX, end to end:** product flows and the full interface for sellers, from the store builder and storefront themes to the supplier marketplace and orders.
- **Build:** the product and the shopinbos.com website, on desktop and mobile, including the product imagery shown here.

See it at [shopinbos.com](https://shopinbos.com/). Our marketing team also worked on its growth: read the [Shopinbos case study](marketing:/case-studies/shopinbos/).
