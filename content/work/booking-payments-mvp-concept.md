This payments MVP concept shows how we'd build a first version for a service business that takes bookings over WhatsApp. See how we scope [MVP development](/services/mvp-development/), browse more [work](/work/), or [start a project](/start/).

## The idea

Service businesses that take bookings over WhatsApp lose time to back-and-forth. This concept shows the first version we'd build: a booking link, a deposit at checkout, and reminders.

## How we'd scope it

One core journey (book, pay a deposit, get a reminder), a simple admin view of the day, and payments through a local provider. Everything else waits for real usage.
